## The Free message allowance

On Free, the web chat allows **20 AI messages every 5 hours**. When you have 4 left, Mazer offers to save your plan; on the last one, it saves the trip and shortlist for you and tells you when messages reset. Nothing you've planned is lost.

You can see what you've used in **Settings → Usage & Limits**.

## The 7-day trial

Pro and Elite both start with a **7-day free trial, with no card required**. During the trial you get the plan's features. Reels and songs are the exception: a trial includes one of each, and the full monthly allowance starts when your subscription does.

## Changing or cancelling

Manage your plan in **Settings → Billing & Plan**. A plan is managed where you bought it, and it works everywhere you sign in: web, Android, WhatsApp and Telegram.

### Plans bought in the Android app

In the Android app, plans are bought through **Google Play**. See [Buying a plan in the Android app](/install/android#buying-a-plan-in-the-app).

- **Upgrading from Pro to Elite** replaces your plan rather than adding a second one. Google Play charges the difference for the rest of the current period, and your renewal date stays the same.
- **Cancelling:** open the **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**, pick TripMazer and tap **Cancel subscription**. You keep the plan until the end of the period you've paid for.
- **Refunds** for Google Play purchases follow Google Play's refund policy. Ask Google from the same Subscriptions page, or write to us and we'll help.

For plans bought anywhere else, see the [cancellation and refunds policy](https://tripmazer.com/legal/cancellation-refunds).
