## The Free message allowance

On Free, the web chat allows **20 AI messages every 5 hours**. When you have 4 left, Mazer offers to save your plan; on the last one, it saves the trip and shortlist for you and tells you when messages reset. Nothing you've planned is lost.

You can see what you've used in **Settings → Usage & Limits**.

## The 7-day trial

Pro and Elite both start with a **7-day free trial, with no card required**. During the trial you get the plan's features. Reels and songs are the exception: a trial includes one of each, and the full monthly allowance starts when your subscription does.

## Changing or cancelling

Manage your plan in **Settings → Billing & Plan**. For refunds, see the [cancellation and refunds policy](https://tripmazer.com/legal/cancellation-refunds).

<Warning>
  Plans can't be bought inside the Android app yet. The trial works there; to subscribe, use [tripmazer.com/pricing](https://tripmazer.com/pricing) in a browser and the plan applies to the app too.
</Warning>
