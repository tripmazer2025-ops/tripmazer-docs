Every trip has an itinerary on its **Plan** screen: one card per day, each stop with its time, price and that day's opening hours, and the walk or ride between stops.

## Getting a plan

There are two ways in:

- **Plan in chat, then save.** Work the plan out with Mazer, then say **"save it"**. The day-by-day plan from the chat goes into the trip's itinerary once, as named stops, as long as it fits the trip's dates. It never overwrites days you've already planned.
- **Let Mazer plan it all.** On a saved trip, say **"plan the whole trip"**. Mazer searches flights, stays and things to do, then fills the days.

## The Day check

Each day has a **Day check** that looks for problems before you hit them:

- clashes and tight connections
- places that are closed when you'd arrive
- long routes and overfull days
- missing meals and no free time

Most problems come with a one-tap fix, and every fix can be undone.

## Pace that follows your energy

Mazer sets each day's pace from your trip's pace, then lightens it for:

- your arrival day
- the day after a long flight, or a time difference of 5 hours or more
- the day after heavy days (a trek counts double, a café barely counts)
- a hot forecast
- you saying you're low today

On the trip, say **"I'm tired"** or **"my feet hurt"** and Mazer offers to lighten the rest of today. Heavier stops move to the lightest later day or are skipped. Meals, bookings and rest stay put, and nothing changes until you tap to apply it (**Lighten my day** on WhatsApp and Telegram).

<Note>Mazer doesn't read wearables or health apps. Pace comes from your trip and what you tell it.</Note>

## Changing a day

You can change any day yourself on the **Plan** screen, or ask Mazer:

Mazer's changes arrive as a card showing what will change. Tap to apply it, or choose **Autopilot** in **Settings → AI Preferences** to have them apply on their own.

**Add Places** on a day ranks places that fit that day's free time and are nearby.

## Plan B for bad days

<Info>Plan B is on **Mazer Pro** and **Mazer Elite**. On Free, Mazer sends a plain weather alert.</Info>

The evening before each trip day, Mazer checks what could spoil it, for the hours you'll actually be out:

- rain or storms over your outdoor stops
- extreme heat or very poor air
- a cyclone, flood or earthquake alert near your destination
- a strike, bandh or closure reported for that day

If the day is at risk, Mazer sends one message with a **Plan B** ready, in the app, on Telegram or on WhatsApp. For a storm or a cyclone it can warn you three days ahead. A Plan B changes as little as it can, in this order:

1. moving a stop to a clear hour the same day
2. moving it to a clear day of your trip, and bringing that day's indoor stops into its place
3. swapping it for a real indoor place nearby that's open at that time, such as a museum or an aquarium

Nothing changes until you tap **Swap to Plan B** (or reply **SWAP** on WhatsApp or Telegram). **Keep my plan** (or **KEEP**) leaves the day as it is, and Mazer won't ask about it again unless something serious changes. You can **Undo** a swap for 24 hours. If you've edited the day since the Plan B was made, Mazer won't overwrite your change.

Booked and locked stops are never moved. For a booked one, Mazer tells you to check its weather or cancellation policy. You get at most one Plan B message a day, and never between 9 PM and 7 AM at your destination.

## Booking links on stops

When Mazer adds an activity, it attaches a checked booking link from partners such as Viator, Tiqets or Headout where one exists, so you can book the tickets from the stop.

## Sharing and exporting

- **Share a read-only link**: ask "give me a share link for this trip". People can view it without an account.
- **Calendar**: ask for a calendar (`.ics`) file, or connect [Google Calendar](/features/connected-apps) and ask Mazer to add the trip.
- **Files**: ask for the itinerary as a PDF or Word document.
- **Itinerary PDF**: on the itinerary, select **Export → PDF Document**. You get a magazine-style PDF: a cover, a contents page, your flights, stays and booked activities, a page for each day with a photo and its plan, emergency numbers, money tips and your packing list, and a QR code that opens the live trip.
  - **Your own photos**: photos you've added to the trip appear on the cover and the last page, and on any day without a map photo. Star a photo to choose it. Without a star, only scenery and big moments are used, never photos of rooms, mirrors, food or paperwork.
  - The PDF is a copy from the day you make it. Export again after changes, or scan the code for the live trip.
