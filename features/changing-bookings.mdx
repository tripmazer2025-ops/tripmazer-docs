Mazer can check whether a booking can change and what it would cost. The change itself is always confirmed by you, and for flights, made by the airline or our flight partner.

## Hotels

Hotels can't edit the dates on a booking, so a date change is a **new booking for the new dates**, confirmed before the original is cancelled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask Mazer to price it">
    <Prompt text="Can I move my [Bali] hotel to [14–18 December]? What would it cost?" />
    Mazer checks whether the booking can move and shows the new cost, the expected refund and the difference, with any cancellation fee stated. Estimates are labelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the change">
    Open the trip's **Bookings** screen and choose **Change dates** on the hotel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Refunds for the original booking come from the hotel provider within 7–10 business days.

<Warning>Pricing a change doesn't make it. Your dates only change once you complete **Change dates**.</Warning>

## Flights

No system lets anyone reissue a ticket automatically, so Mazer never changes a flight itself. It checks your booking and tells you who can change it, and at what cost.

<Prompt text="I need to move my [Delhi] flight to [the next day]. What are my options?" />

Depending on the airline and fare, the options are:

1. **Our flight partner reissues it** for the fare difference, plus the airline's change fee, plus a USD 25 servicing fee, charged only if you accept. Mazer can email the partner's flight desk for you, copying you in, once you approve it on a confirmation card. Replies take up to 72 hours, 48 hours inside three weeks of the flight, and 4–6 hours inside a week.
2. **Change it yourself on the airline's site.** IndiGo, Akasa, SpiceJet and Air India Express allow this, with no USD 25 fee.
3. **Some fares can't change**, and within about 48 hours of departure only the airline can help.

TripMazer adds no fee of its own. A changed flight doesn't update by itself: our team records it from the partner's confirmation, or you enter it in **Bookings → Change Flight**.

## Bookings made elsewhere

Bookings you made outside TripMazer (and imported) are changed through whoever sold them.
