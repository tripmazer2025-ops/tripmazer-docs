<Info>Negotiate for Me is on **Mazer Elite**, on the web at [tripmazer.com/tools/negotiate](https://tripmazer.com/tools/negotiate).</Info>

Negotiate for Me turns a hotel or airline booking into a **case** with a clear goal: an upgrade, a refund, a discount, compensation or a fee waiver. Mazer writes the email or call script to make your case.

## How a case works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a case">
    Go to **Negotiate for Me**, choose the booking and what you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Mazer writes an email, or a script if you'd rather call. Edit it as you like.

    <AppShot src="/images/app/negotiate-letter.webp" alt="A Negotiate for Me email asking a Bali villa for an anniversary upgrade and late checkout, with tone and length controls" width={480} caption="An upgrade request for an anniversary stay." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Send it from your Gmail, or copy it. Nothing is sent until you tap **Send**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the replies">
    For cases sent from Gmail, Mazer checks for replies every few hours. When one arrives, it's added to the case, compared with your goal, and you're told. After 2 quiet days, Mazer reminds you that a nudge is due.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Cases live on the Negotiate for Me page. Mazer in chat can't see them or their replies, so check the page for the latest.
</Note>
