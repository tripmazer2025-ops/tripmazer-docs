## Mazer Cuts

<Info>Mazer Cuts is on **Mazer Elite**, including the Elite trial.</Info>

Mazer Cuts makes reels from your conversation with Mazer, cut to the beat of any song, with:

- captions, translated if you like
- voiceover
- motion titles and maps
- photo moments
- HD export, sized for Instagram, plus project files you can open in a video editor

<Prompt text="Make a beat-cut reel of [Bali] to [an upbeat song], with captions in [Hindi]." />

On the web, a live reel card shows its progress. A reel isn't ready until it arrives.

## Songs

Mazer can write and produce an original song: sung vocals, a rap, a jingle or an instrumental. Describe the genre, mood or title, or give it your own lyrics and they'll be sung as written. Each request gives you two variations.

| Plan | Songs a month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 5 |
| Elite | 18 |

A trial includes one song.

<Prompt text="Write a song about our [Goa] trip, [upbeat Bollywood] style, and mention [the sunset at Chapora]." />

Naming an artist guides the style only. Mazer never imitates anyone's voice.

## AI video

On the web, Mazer can make short AI videos (4–8 second vertical reels, or 16:9 cinematic shots) with ambient sound and dialogue, and animate a still photo.

<Prompt text="Animate this photo of [the beach at sunset] into a short vertical clip." />

For an existing video, Mazer can trim it, change its speed, mute it, reframe it, caption it or adjust its colour directly. Changing what happens in a scene is done by re-rendering it, not by editing the original footage.

## Images

Mazer can generate images from a description and edit images you upload or it made:

- **Quick changes without a re-render**: crop, resize, rotate, flip, colour and format
- **Changes to part of an image**, such as removing an object
- **Restyling a whole image**, using yours as the reference

<Prompt text="Make this photo look like a vintage travel postcard." />
