Flights and hotels book inside TripMazer. Mazer searches, compares and explains; **Book** on a flight or hotel card opens our checkout, where you add travellers and extras and pay. Mazer never pays on its own.

## Searching

<Prompt title="Flights" text="Find flights from [Bengaluru] to [Bali] on [12 December], back on [16 December], for 2 adults. No red-eyes." />

<Prompt title="Hotels" text="Hotels in [Seminyak] under [₹8,000] a night, free cancellation, walking distance to the beach." />

How Mazer searches:

- **Return trips are searched one leg at a time**, so you see the best options each way.
- **Mazer only quotes fares and rooms a search actually returned.** It won't invent a price, airline or currency.
- **Your budget guides the ranking but isn't a hidden filter.** Only limits you state (like "under ₹8,000 a night") filter results. If everything costs more than your budget, Mazer says so in real numbers and suggests fewer nights, other dates or a cheaper area.
- **"Nothing free on those dates"** means the hotel has no rooms then, not that the search failed.

## Hotel prices

The hotel card shows the price per night **before tax** and the total **including tax**, so you can compare it with other sites line for line. If a hotel collects a fee or tax on arrival, the card shows that amount too.

### Member prices

Hotel prices depend on your plan: **Pro** pays less than Free, and **Elite** pays the least. This applies everywhere you search, including WhatsApp and Telegram. On Free, the hotel card shows what the same stay costs on Pro.

Flights cost the same on every plan.

### Price check

On the web, TripMazer checks Booking.com, Agoda and Trip.com for the same hotel and nights after the card appears:

- **Lowest price we found**: our price is lower than theirs. The card lists what they charge.
- **Matched to [site]**: another site was cheaper, so we lowered our price to just under theirs. The matched price holds through checkout for about two hours.

Not every hotel can be compared, and when we can't match a price, the card shows nothing. You need to be signed in for the price check.

## Stays on partner sites

We can't sell rooms everywhere. In some places, such as smaller Japanese onsen towns, we can book few stays or none. Mazer then shows stays priced on other sites on the same card, marked **Partner price**.

These stays can't be booked in TripMazer. **Book on Booking.com** (or **Google Hotels**) opens that site with your dates and party filled in, and you book and pay there. Prices are estimates and may rise once the site adds taxes. You can still add the stay to your itinerary with **+**. If you book one, TripMazer won't show it as booked unless you add the confirmation to your trip.

For a group, partner prices are per room, and the card counts the rooms your group needs (two people a room) in the estimated total.

Want a whole house or villa rather than hotel rooms? Ask for one and Mazer [finds homes on Airbnb](/features/files-research-and-browsing#homes-on-airbnb).

## Trains and buses

Ask about trains or buses between two places and Mazer reads the timetables before it answers, so the train numbers, times and fares it gives are real.

<Prompt text="Trains from [Thrissur] to [Bangalore] on [19 December]." />

- **Trains in India** come from the Indian Railways timetable: every train between the stations, its running days, departure and arrival, journey time and the listed fare for each class. If there's no direct train, Mazer shows a route with one change and the days it works.
- **Buses** come from FlixBus, live: departure and drop points, sleeper or seat, the price and seats left. Other operators, such as state transport and private sleepers, aren't included; Mazer links a redBus search for those.
- Trains and buses aren't booked in TripMazer. Seats, final fares and booking are on IRCTC for trains and FlixBus or redBus for buses.

## Checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Book">
    On the card in chat. On WhatsApp and Telegram, Mazer sends a checkout link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Travellers">
    Add everyone travelling. Names must match passports or IDs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extras">
    Choose any extras the fare or room offers. For flights that is seats and extra bags, and, when the airline sells them, meals, lounge access, priority, Wi-Fi and special assistance. Each traveller and each flight has its own picks. All of them are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Pay in the checkout. Your booking appears on the trip's **Bookings** screen with its confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you book

- **Bookings** on the trip shows every flight, hotel and activity, including ones [imported from email](/features/importing-bookings).
- **Free cancellation reminders**: on WhatsApp and Telegram, Mazer reminds you 3 days before a hotel's free cancellation ends.
- **Cheaper rooms**: if a refundable room you booked with us gets cheaper, it's flagged on **Bookings** for one-tap rebooking.
- **Changes**: see [Changing a booking](/features/changing-bookings).

## Watching prices

Ask Mazer to watch a fare and it tells you only when the price drops below what you set. Flight price watches are on Pro (10 routes) and Elite (unlimited).

<Prompt title="Watch a fare" text="Watch fares from [BLR] to [DPS] for [December] and tell me if they drop below [₹18,000]." />

On Elite, the **Price Calendar** ([tripmazer.com/tools/price-calendar](https://tripmazer.com/tools/price-calendar)) shows a route's fares for every day of the month, so the cheapest days stand out.

## Cancellations and refunds

Each fare and room shows its cancellation terms before you book. TripMazer can't override a non-refundable fare or rate. Hotel refunds come from the hotel provider, usually within 7–10 business days. See the [cancellation and refunds policy](https://tripmazer.com/legal/cancellation-refunds).
