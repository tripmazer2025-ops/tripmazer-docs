Flights and hotels book inside TripMazer. Mazer searches, compares and explains; **Book** on a flight or hotel card opens our checkout, where you add travellers and extras and pay. Mazer never pays on its own.

## Searching

<Prompt title="Flights" text="Find flights from [Bengaluru] to [Bali] on [12 December], back on [16 December], for 2 adults. No red-eyes." />

<Prompt title="Hotels" text="Hotels in [Seminyak] under [₹8,000] a night, free cancellation, walking distance to the beach." />

How Mazer searches:

- **Return trips are searched one leg at a time**, so you see the best options each way.
- **Mazer only quotes fares and rooms a search actually returned.** It won't invent a price, airline or currency.
- **Your budget guides the ranking but isn't a hidden filter.** Only limits you state (like "under ₹8,000 a night") filter results. If everything costs more than your budget, Mazer says so in real numbers and suggests fewer nights, other dates or a cheaper area.
- **"Nothing free on those dates"** means the hotel has no rooms then, not that the search failed.

## Checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Book">
    On the card in chat. On WhatsApp and Telegram, Mazer sends a checkout link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Travellers">
    Add everyone travelling. Names must match passports or IDs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extras">
    Choose any extras the fare or room offers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Pay in the checkout. Your booking appears on the trip's **Bookings** screen with its confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you book

- **Bookings** on the trip shows every flight, hotel and activity, including ones [imported from email](/features/importing-bookings).
- **Free cancellation reminders**: on WhatsApp and Telegram, Mazer reminds you 3 days before a hotel's free cancellation ends.
- **Cheaper rooms**: if a refundable room you booked with us gets cheaper, it's flagged on **Bookings** for one-tap rebooking.
- **Changes**: see [Changing a booking](/features/changing-bookings).

## Watching prices

Ask Mazer to watch a fare and it tells you only when the price drops below what you set. Flight price watches are on Pro (10 routes) and Elite (unlimited).

<Prompt title="Watch a fare" text="Watch fares from [BLR] to [DPS] for [December] and tell me if they drop below [₹18,000]." />

## Cancellations and refunds

Each fare and room shows its cancellation terms before you book. TripMazer can't override a non-refundable fare or rate. Hotel refunds come from the hotel provider, usually within 7–10 business days. See the [cancellation and refunds policy](https://tripmazer.com/legal/cancellation-refunds).
