## What the trip will cost

The trip's **Budget** screen shows the cost by category against your budget, as a likely range. It includes:

- prices for rooms and fares you haven't booked yet, estimated when you open it on Pro and Elite
- destination fees, such as Dubai's Tourism Dirham
- your on-trip daily allowance, group shares and who owes whom

From the Budget screen you can book, watch fares, and **fit the trip to your budget**. Mazer suggests real-price swaps, the same thing for less, up to one step down in quality, and applies them only when you approve.

## Logging spend

On WhatsApp and Telegram, type what you spent or send a receipt photo. Mazer logs it to the active trip and replies with today's balance.

<Prompt title="Typed" text="Tuk tuk [800 LKR]" />

<Prompt title="Several at once" text="Lunch [₹1,200], museum [₹600], auto [₹180]." />

Expenses in any currency are converted to your home currency for the totals.

## Spend pace

Once a trip of three days or more is under way and has a budget and at least three logged expenses, Mazer projects your spending to the last day. If you're heading more than about 20% over budget, it warns you while there's still time, with a daily amount that would get you back on track. It also tells you if you're well under budget. Each alert fires once, and the settings are in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Trip money

For trips abroad, the Trip Home has three money tools:

- **Money plan**: the exchange rate, which of your saved cards adds the least in fees, and UPI where it works.
- **Card payments**: paste a bank SMS or email and it becomes an expense once you tick it. Turn on **Find trip spends and refunds in Gmail** and Mazer reads your bank's own alerts too. With **Add trip spends automatically** on, spends in the trip's country are added straight away, and you can undo them in Budget.
- **Refunds and claims**: track money you're owed, ticked off when the refund lands. **Chase** asks Mazer to draft a follow-up.
