<Info>Building tools is on **Mazer Elite**.</Info>

Ask Mazer for a small app and it builds one for you, in TripMazer's own style. It keeps its own data, so it remembers where you left off. Your tools live in **More tools → Mazer Makes**, and the ones you make in a trip can be shared with everyone on it.

<Prompt text="Build me Japanese phrase flashcards for my [Kyoto] trip, with a streak." />

<Prompt text="Make us a scorekeeper for [UNO] on our [Goa] trip, with a running total for each of us." />

<Prompt text="Build me a tool that converts [shoe and clothing sizes] between [India], the [US] and [Japan]." />

## What you can build

Anything that works on its own data, travel or not:

- flashcards and quizzes, like phrases for your next trip
- scorekeepers and shared lists for the group
- calculators and converters
- logs for a hobby: dives, hikes, coffee, wine
- timers and light games

TripMazer already handles packing lists, trip money and splits, group polls, goals and habits, and the trip countdown. Ask for one of those and Mazer points you to it instead of building a copy.

Tools can use live information. Mazer's own weather, exchange rates and your trips need no setup. A tool can also read from a public website, but the first time it wants a new site, TripMazer asks you: **Allow** or **Don't allow**. Your account, cookies and other data are never sent.

## How it's built

Building takes about a minute. Mazer writes the tool, checks it, then opens it in a real browser at phone size, in light and dark mode. It fixes anything that's broken before you see it. In chat, a card shows the progress and then the tool itself. On WhatsApp and Telegram, you get a message with a link when it's ready.

## Changing a tool

Say what you want in your own words, and Mazer builds the next version. Your saved data stays.

<Prompt text="Change my [flashcards] to add numbers and a quiz mode." />

In **Mazer Makes**, open a tool and use **⋯** to:

- **Share with the trip** (or stop sharing), for tools made inside a trip
- **Go back to an earlier version**
- **Remove** the tool

Mazer can also read a tool's data back to you.

## Limits

- **What tools can't do:** they can't ask for passwords, card details or one-time codes, take payments, collect other people's personal details, or imitate another company's site. Mazer turns those down and suggests something it can build instead.
- **How they run:** each tool runs walled off from the rest of TripMazer. It can only reach the internet through TripMazer, and only to the sites you allowed.
- **How much:** up to 25 tools, 10 builds or changes a day, and up to 2 MB of saved data per tool.
