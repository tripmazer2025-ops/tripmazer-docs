Bookings you made outside TripMazer belong in your trip too, so Mazer can answer "what time is my check-in?" and plan around them.

## Gmail auto-import

<Info>Gmail auto-import is on **Mazer Pro** and **Elite**.</Info>

Connect Gmail in **Settings → Integrations**. From then on, every ten minutes Mazer files the booking emails it's sure of (airline, hotel, rail and activity confirmations) into the matching trip, creating a trip if there isn't one. Each becomes a booking card, an expense and an itinerary line, never a duplicate.

- **Changes** to a booking update it, and **cancellations** cancel it.
- **Marketing emails are never imported.** Emails Mazer isn't sure about wait as suggestions for you to confirm.
- **Each run sends you one message** listing what was added. Any import can be undone for 7 days.
- **On first connect, Mazer reads two years of email.** Upcoming bookings are filed into trips. Past ones become past trips for you to review on **My Trips** and in Settings.

## Forward or upload

On any plan:

- **WhatsApp and Telegram**: forward the confirmation email or send the PDF to Mazer.
- **Web**: attach the PDF or a screenshot in chat, or paste the confirmation text.

## Documents

Tickets, vouchers, visas and IDs live on the trip's **Docs** screen, organised per traveller. On the [Android app](/install/android), **Lock Docs** puts them behind your phone's screen lock.

## Disconnecting Gmail

Disconnecting Gmail deletes its inbox-scan previews. Bookings already imported stay in your trips.
