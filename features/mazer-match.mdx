**Mazer Match** helps you find other travellers: people visiting the same place on overlapping dates, or, on Elite, people close to you right now. It's opt-in, and nobody can message you until you've both agreed to connect.

Open it from **Mazer Match** in the sidebar, or from a trip to see matches for that trip.

| Plan | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Same Trip matches, with 2 connection requests a day |
| Pro | Same Trip matches, with unlimited connection requests |
| Elite | Everything in Pro, plus **Near You** |

## Set up your profile

The first time, you create a travel profile: a photo, your travel style, pace, budget and interests. Links to your Instagram, LinkedIn or X are optional, and are only visible after you connect with someone.

## Same Trip and Near You

- **Same Trip** shows travellers whose trips overlap yours.
- **Near You** (Elite) shows who's close to you now, within a search area you choose.

## Connecting

Send a connection request to someone you'd like to meet. Once they accept, you can chat in TripMazer without sharing a phone number.

## Plan together

Once connected, **Together** gives you a shared space for:

- **Places**: what you've both saved
- **Meetups**: suggest a time and place to meet
- **Expenses**: split shared costs, such as a taxi or a meal

## Your privacy and safety

Near You asks for your location first, and works like this:

- **Others see a rough distance**, such as "~5 km away", never your exact location.
- **Precise coordinates are deleted daily.** Only your city is kept.
- **Ghost mode** lets you browse nearby without appearing to others. Your Same Trip matches stay active.

In **You on Mazer Match**, turn off **Discoverable** to stop showing your profile to travellers who match. You can also block or report someone at any time. Revoke location access in your browser or device settings.

<Warning>Meet new people in public places, and tell someone you trust where you're going. [Check on me](/features/safety-and-family#check-on-me) can help.</Warning>
