## Calculations, charts and files

For anything code does better than prose, Mazer writes and runs a small program. That covers bill splits, expense totals, currency and date maths, and analysing your own trips, expenses, itinerary, bookings, budgets and packing.

It can hand you:

- **charts**, as images in chat (photos on WhatsApp and Telegram)
- **files**: PDF, Excel or CSV, Word, calendar (`.ics`) and QR codes

<Prompt text="Make me an Excel file of every expense on my [Goa] trip, by category, with a chart of spending per day." />

<Prompt text="Turn my [Japan] itinerary into a calendar file I can import." />

Programs can look up exchange rates, weather, places, events and flight status. They work on a read-only copy of your data and can never send, book, save or delete. Each run lasts about a minute.

| Plan | Runs a day |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5 |
| Pro | 30 |
| Elite | 100 |

## Documents you can keep

Ask Mazer to save research, checklists, comparisons, drafts or presentations as documents. You can reopen and revise them later. Mazer edits the document in place and keeps the previous version, so you never need to re-send a file it made.

Everything Mazer has made for you is in your **Library** ([tripmazer.com/library](https://tripmazer.com/library)): documents, spreadsheets, presentations, images, videos and music.

<Prompt text="Save a packing checklist for [Iceland in February] as a document." />

## Deep research

For questions that need depth, Mazer writes a **cited report** from the live web, your memory and your saved documents, and saves it as a document. On the web it replies with a short answer and the report. On WhatsApp and Telegram the report arrives in the chat 2–5 minutes later, so you don't need to wait.

<Prompt text="Do deep research on the best time to see the northern lights in [Norway] vs [Finland], with costs." />

Ordinary questions get a direct answer, not a research run.

## Reading and using websites

Paste a link and Mazer reads, summarises or compares the page. Give it a goal, with or without a link, and it searches, reads pages across sites, uses menus and site search, and downloads matching files.

<Prompt text="Find [Emirates]' baggage allowance for economy to [Dubai] and summarise it." />

- **Forms are filled only from details you gave.** Mazer shows you what it entered and submits only after you confirm.
- **It stops at logins, payment, one-time codes and ID details.** On **Elite**, at a login, CAPTCHA or code, Mazer sends you a private 30-minute link to sign in on the live page yourself. Mazer never sees what you type, and continues once you're in. Sessions are saved encrypted in **Settings → Connected Websites**.
- **Payment always stops with you.**

The one place Mazer signs in by itself is an approved [Elite cancellation](/features/life-admin#elite-mazer-cancels-it-for-you), with the login you saved in **Settings → Saved Passwords**.

## Web tasks in the background

A goal that takes longer than the reply carries on in the background, for up to about 5 minutes, and posts the answer in the same chat when it's done. You can keep chatting meanwhile.

<Prompt text="On [goindigo.in], find the cabin bag limit for [international flights]." />

- On the web, each step shows with a screenshot, and **Stop** ends the task. On **Elite**, **Watch live** shows the page as Mazer works (you can watch, not control it). On Telegram, progress comes as messages with a **Stop** button.
- **Files it downloads** go to your **Library**, and arrive as documents on WhatsApp and Telegram.
- **Ask for a report**, such as "…and give me a PDF" or "…as a Word report", and Mazer turns what it found into a document. A report counts as a document on your plan.
- Background tasks only read and search. They don't sign in, fill forms or buy anything.

| Free | The quick read in the reply only |
| Pro | 5 |
| Elite | 25 |

## Site watches

Name a site and Mazer watches that page, opening it on every check and messaging you only when what you asked about changes. The first check tells you what it sees now.

<Prompt text="Watch [the visa page on vfsglobal.com] and tell me when [new appointment dates] appear." />

| Plan | Site watches | How often they check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | 2 | Daily |
| Elite | 10 | Hourly |

Site watches don't count towards your daily web tasks. Each one is listed in **Settings → Agent Tasks** as a **Site watch**, where **Check now** runs it straight away. On Free, or past your limit, Mazer sets up an ordinary [watch](/features/watches-routines-and-tasks#watches) instead and says so.
