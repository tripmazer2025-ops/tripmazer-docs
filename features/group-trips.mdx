## Bringing people in

<Prompt title="Invite" text="Give me an invite link so [Priya] can join the [Goa] trip." />

People who join open the trip from their own TripMazer account. The trip's **Group** screen shows who's coming.

To share without inviting, send a read-only link. Anyone can view it without an account, and booking references are never on it.

<Prompt title="Share link" text="Give me a share link for the [Goa] trip." />

<Prompt title="Send the plan" text="Send my [Goa] itinerary to [Mum]." />

## Deciding together

- **Shortlists**: on shared trips, Explore's hearts show who saved what.
- **Split plans**: when the group wants different things, ask Mazer for a split plan for that day.
- **Telegram groups**: add @TripMazerBot to the group chat and use `/linktrip`. You get polls (`/newpoll`), `/split`, `/status` and `/who`, and Mazer answers when it's mentioned. See [Telegram](/install/telegram#use-mazer-in-a-telegram-group).

<Prompt text="Half of us want the beach on day [2] and half want the old town. Make a split plan for that day." />

## Money

The Budget screen shows group shares and who owes whom. Log shared costs by telling Mazer who paid, and connect Splitwise if your group already uses it. See [Budget and expenses](/features/budget-and-expenses).

## Meeting other travellers

Looking for company rather than planning with friends? See [Mazer Match](/features/mazer-match).

## Photos

Group trips can have a shared album that everyone opts into, with **Leave me out** for anyone who'd rather not be in it, and a group film at the end. See [Memories](/features/memories).
