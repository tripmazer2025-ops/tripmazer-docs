## Watches

<Prompt text="Watch fares from [BLR] to [DPS] for [December] and tell me if they drop below [₹18,000]." />

A watch checks on a schedule and messages you **only** when something changes or your condition is met: a fare under your threshold, slots opening up, a rule changing. A quiet watch is still working.

| Plan | How often watches check |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Daily |
| Pro | Every 6 hours |
| Elite | Every 15 minutes |

Watches that stay quiet for a long time check less often. To have Mazer open a particular website on every check, use a [site watch](/features/files-research-and-browsing#site-watches).

## Deal Radar

Deal Radar watches the places you care about and tells you when flights from your home airport, or hotels there, are well below their usual price.

<Prompt text="Add [Bali] to my deal radar." />

| Plan | Places | How often it checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | 5 | Daily |
| Elite | 10 | Every 8 hours |

Set it up in **Settings → Agent Tasks → Deal Radar**, or in chat: your home airport, your places, whether to watch flights, hotels or both, and how far below usual a price must be (20%, 30%, 40% or 50%; 30% by default). A new place stays quiet for about two weeks while Mazer learns its usual price, so the first alert can take a while.

## Errands

<Prompt text="Get my refund from [the hotel] for the cancelled night. Follow up until it's sorted." />

An errand is an outcome Mazer works towards over days: a refund, a follow-up with a hotel or airline, a confirmation. It checks on a schedule, reads whole email threads, and prepares next steps (emails, calendar events, Notion, Docs or Drive), each waiting for your approval.

- **Limited steps.** Mazer prepares only the kinds and number of steps you allow: 5 by default, up to 25. Say "give it 5 more" to extend it.
- **Price limits are firm.** In a negotiation, Mazer never exceeds or reveals your limit, and only you can change it.
- **It ends on its own** when the goal is met or its end date passes (7 days by default, up to 30). Say "stop that errand" to end it early.
- **It never posts publicly or pays.**

## Scheduled tasks

<Prompt text="Every Monday at 8 am, send me a digest of deals for [Japan] in [April]." />

Scheduled tasks run on their own and report back: daily, on a chosen weekday, or every few minutes (at least 5). They read web search, your memory and past conversations, bookings, events, and your connected apps. Ask for one that prepares changes, like a reply or a calendar entry, and each change waits for your approval.

Create, list and cancel them in conversation. They're also listed in **Settings → Agent Tasks**, where you can pause or delete them.

### Tasks that act on their own

<Info>Tasks that act on their own are on **Mazer Elite**.</Info>

A scheduled task can also make the changes itself, in any app you've connected: Gmail, Outlook, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Notion, Todoist, LinkedIn, X and the rest, including your own MCP servers. Say you want it done, not just reported.

<Prompt text="Every Friday, label my newsletters [Newsletters] and archive them, and do it without asking." />

<Prompt text="Every evening, add tomorrow's bookings to my calendar and to my [Trips] project in Todoist." />

1. **A dry run first.** Mazer runs the task straight away without changing anything, and shows you what it would have done ("archive 34 emails, add 2 events") and what it may do each time.
2. **You approve once.** Tap **Let it run** (on WhatsApp, reply **APPROVE**). From the next run it does those things on its own.
3. **Each run tells you what it did**, with **Undo**. Undo works for 7 days (on WhatsApp, reply **UNDO** within a day).

What it may do is fixed when you approve:

- **Only the same places.** It stays on the label, sheet, page, project or calendar it used in the dry run.
- **Only people you approved.** It sends or posts only to the people and accounts in the dry run. Anyone new, and any reply to whoever wrote, waits for your OK.
- **Deletes only if they can be undone**, like archiving or removing a label. A permanent delete always asks.
- **Never** booking, paying, calling, or anything through the browser.
- **A limit per run**, about twice what the dry run did. If a run would do far more than usual, Mazer pauses the task and tells you.

Anything outside that waits for your approval, as before. Pause, resume or revoke a task's permission in **Settings → Agent Tasks → Acting on its own**. An approval lasts 90 days, then Mazer asks again.

## Routines

<Prompt text="Save this as a routine." />

Mazer proposes the steps from the last few hours with a name and trigger phrases, and saves the routine once you approve. Saying a phrase runs it again; on Telegram, it also gets its own `/command`. Mazer may offer, once, to save something you've done on several days.

A routine pre-approves nothing: steps that send, book, pay or call still ask each time. Keep up to 20 routines, managed in **Settings → Skills**.

## The Activity log

**Settings → Activity** shows the last 30 days of Mazer's work on every channel:

- what it did
- approvals waiting for you (approve or decline them there or in chat)
- errands and watches in progress
- bookings made through Mazer

## Skills

**Settings → Skills** lists Mazer's specialist skills, such as pre-trip readiness, visa and entry, arrival kit, flight disruption monitor, expense reconciler, points and loyalty, call ahead, meeting prep and automatic check-in. Mazer doesn't use a skill you've turned off. Skills that read Gmail, Drive, Docs or Calendar need Pro or Elite.
