## Live interpreter and bargaining

On Telegram, `/interpret English Thai` starts a two-way live interpreter, and `/negotiate` starts a bargaining helper with fair prices and cultural tips. `/stopinterp` ends either one. When a session ends, Mazer offers to log the price, add places, set reminders or save details.

Anywhere else, just ask:

<Prompt text="Translate this menu and tell me what's vegetarian." />

<Prompt text="What's a fair taxi fare from here to [the airport], and how should I bargain?" />

## Phrase packs

<Prompt text="Give me the 20 phrases I'll actually need in [Vietnam], with pronunciation." />

On Telegram, `/phrases` makes an offline phrase pack for any language or destination.

## Trip Audio Guide

Mazer narrates your itinerary stop by stop, in 18 languages. It's made automatically 3 days before a trip, or ask for it any time.

<Prompt text="Make an audio guide for my [Kyoto] trip in [English]." />

## Call ahead

<Info>Call ahead is on **Mazer Elite** and uses real call minutes.</Info>

Once you approve, Mazer places a **real phone call** to a hotel, restaurant or airline, in the local language or one you choose. The caller says it's an AI, never gives card details, and never agrees to a charge. Afterwards Mazer reports exactly what happened.

<Prompt text="Call [Hotel Name] and ask if we can check in early at [11 am] tomorrow." />

## Visa appointment watch

<Info>Visa appointment watch is on **Mazer Elite**, for US and Schengen visas.</Info>

Mazer reads public appointment-slot trackers and alerts you when a slot you could use opens up. It never books the appointment.

<Prompt text="Watch for US visa interview slots in [Mumbai] before [March]." />

## Mazer Radio

**Mazer Radio** is TripMazer's weekly show: one city per episode, with original songs and a day there you can save as a plan. It's also on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube.
