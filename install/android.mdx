## What the app adds

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Push notifications" icon="bell">
    Flight alerts, reminders and messages from Mazer arrive as notifications, even when the app is closed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share to Mazer" icon="share-nodes">
    Share a reel, a link, a screenshot or a booking PDF from any app straight into a chat with Mazer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trip mode" icon="location-dot">
    On a trip, the app notices when you reach a stop or your hotel and opens that stop's guide when you arrive.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trip widget" icon="table-cells-large">
    A home-screen widget with what's next on your trip.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Docs lock" icon="lock">
    Lock your passports, visas and tickets behind your fingerprint or screen lock.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Photo import and Wallet passes" icon="images">
    Bring trip photos into Memories, and add flights, stays and activities to Google Wallet.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The app also has a screen-off guide for listening while you walk.

## Signing in

Open the app, and sign in with the same Google, Facebook, Microsoft or email account you use on the web. Your trips, memory and connected apps are already there.

## Reporting a reply

If something Mazer wrote or made is offensive, harmful or wrong, open the reply's menu and tap **Report**. Pick a reason, add a note if you like, and send it. We review every report.

## Buying a plan in the app

Mazer Pro and Mazer Elite are bought in the app through **Google Play**, with the payment method on your Google account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan and billing** and tap **Upgrade plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose **Pro** or **Elite**, monthly or yearly. The price shown is what Google Play charges you, in your currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in Google Play">
    Google Play shows the total before you pay. Your plan switches on within a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Plans renew automatically until you cancel. To cancel, open the **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**, pick TripMazer and tap **Cancel subscription**. You keep the plan until the end of the period you've paid for. More in [Plans and limits](/plans#plans-bought-in-the-android-app).

<Tip>
  Need more reels this month? **Settings → Usage and limits → Top up 5 reels** buys five more through Google Play.
</Tip>

- **Already have a plan?** It applies in the app straight away, wherever you bought it, and the plan screen tells you where to manage it.
- **New phone, or reinstalled?** Sign in with the same account. If your plan doesn't show, tap **Restore purchases** under **Upgrade plan**.
- **Don't see plans with prices?** Update TripMazer from Google Play: buying in the app needs version 1.0.25 or later.
