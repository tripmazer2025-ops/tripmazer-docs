## What the app adds

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Push notifications" icon="bell">
    Flight alerts, reminders and messages from Mazer arrive as notifications, even when the app is closed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share to Mazer" icon="share-nodes">
    Share a reel, a link, a screenshot or a booking PDF from any app straight into a chat with Mazer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trip mode" icon="location-dot">
    On a trip, the app notices when you reach a stop or your hotel and opens that stop's guide when you arrive.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trip widget" icon="table-cells-large">
    A home-screen widget with what's next on your trip.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Docs lock" icon="lock">
    Lock your passports, visas and tickets behind your fingerprint or screen lock.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Photo import and Wallet passes" icon="images">
    Bring trip photos into Memories, and add flights, stays and activities to Google Wallet.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The app also has a screen-off guide for listening while you walk.

## Signing in

Open the app, and sign in with the same Google, Facebook, Microsoft or email account you use on the web. Your trips, memory and connected apps are already there.

## Reporting a reply

If something Mazer wrote or made is offensive, harmful or wrong, open the reply's menu and tap **Report**. Pick a reason, add a note if you like, and send it. We review every report.

## Plans in the app

Plans and credits can't be bought inside the app yet. The [7-day trial](/plans#the-7-day-trial) works there, and a plan you already have applies in the app straight away.
