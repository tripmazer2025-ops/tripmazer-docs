Once WhatsApp is linked, Mazer is a contact in your chat list. It knows your trips, memory and bookings, and it can do most of what it does on the web: plan, search, change the itinerary, log expenses, set reminders, read links and voice notes, and send you alerts.

<Note>WhatsApp and Telegram are part of [Mazer Pro and Elite](/plans), including the 7-day trial.</Note>

## Link WhatsApp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the web app, go to **Settings → Integrations** and find **WhatsApp**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Connect">
    TripMazer opens WhatsApp with a message already typed: **"Hi! Link my WhatsApp:"** followed by a one-time code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    Send it as it is. Don't edit the code. The link expires after 15 minutes; if it does, tap **Connect** again for a fresh one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it's linked">
    Back in **Settings → Integrations**, WhatsApp shows your name or number as linked. Mazer replies in WhatsApp to say hello.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Do this on your phone if you can. On a computer, the Connect button opens WhatsApp Web or the desktop app instead, which works too.
</Tip>

## What to send Mazer

| Send | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A question | “What's my hotel address tonight?” gets answered from your trip |
| A voice note | Mazer listens, answers, and can reply with a voice note in your chosen voice |
| A receipt photo | Logged as an expense on your active trip, with today's balance |
| A typed expense | “tuk tuk 800 LKR” works the same way |
| A booking PDF or forwarded email | Added to the right trip |
| A reel, YouTube or TikTok link | Mazer reads it and pulls out the places, which it can add to a trip |
| A photo of a building or sign | Mazer identifies the place if it can confirm it, and says so if it can't |
| Your location | “What's good near me?” uses it |

Type **/** to open the menu of quick actions.

## Messages from Mazer

On WhatsApp, Mazer can also message you first: a morning digest on trip days, reminders you've set, flight alerts (Elite, if enabled), and check-ins before departure and after landing.

## Unlink WhatsApp

Go to **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp** and choose **Disconnect**. Your trips and memory stay in your account.

## Limits on WhatsApp

- **Groups**: Mazer can't be added to WhatsApp groups. For a group chat with Mazer in it, use [Telegram](/install/telegram#use-mazer-in-a-telegram-group).
- **Checkout**: booking opens TripMazer's checkout as a link; payment always happens there.
- **Rich screens**: the itinerary editor, Memories and Explore are web screens. Mazer sends a link when you need one.
