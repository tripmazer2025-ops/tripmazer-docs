TripMazer runs in any modern browser at [tripmazer.com/app](https://tripmazer.com/app). Nothing to download: sign in and start talking to Mazer.

The web app is the most complete version of TripMazer. Checkout, the trip dashboard, the itinerary's Day check, Memories, Explore and every setting live here.

## Add TripMazer to your iPhone home screen

There's no App Store app yet. Instead, add the web app to your home screen: it opens full-screen, without the browser bar, and stays signed in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open TripMazer in Safari">
    Go to [tripmazer.com/app](https://tripmazer.com/app) in **Safari** and sign in. (Other iPhone browsers can't add web apps to the home screen as reliably.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Share">
    Tap the **Share** button, the square with an arrow pointing up, in Safari's toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
    Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it from your home screen">
    TripMazer now has its own icon. Open it from there rather than from Safari.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  On the move, most people pair the home-screen app with [WhatsApp](/install/whatsapp) or [Telegram](/install/telegram): plan in the app, ask quick questions in chat.
</Tip>

## Install on a computer

In Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC, you can install TripMazer as a desktop app: open [tripmazer.com/app](https://tripmazer.com/app) and choose the **install** icon at the right of the address bar. Otherwise, a pinned browser tab works just as well.

## What works where

| Feature | Web and home-screen app |
| --- | --- |
| Planning chat, trips and itinerary | ✓ |
| Flight and hotel checkout | ✓ |
| Mazer Live voice | ✓ |
| Memories, reels and the trip story | ✓ |
| Push notifications | Use the [Android app](/install/android), WhatsApp or Telegram for alerts on the go |
