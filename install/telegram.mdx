Mazer on Telegram is **@TripMazerBot**. It has everything Mazer does on WhatsApp, plus slash commands, group chats, a live "thinking" view while it works, and sticker packs.

<Note>
  Telegram is part of [Mazer Pro and Elite](/plans). On Free, **Settings → Integrations** shows **Upgrade** instead of **Connect**. If your plan ends later, Telegram stays linked, but Mazer only replies with a way to upgrade until you're back on a paid plan.
</Note>

## Link Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the web app, go to **Settings → Integrations** and find **Telegram**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Connect">
    TripMazer opens **@TripMazerBot** in Telegram.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Start">
    Tap **Start** at the bottom of the chat. The web app updates to show Telegram as linked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="The Telegram link didn't open">
  Some networks block `t.me` links. Instead:

  1. Open Telegram on your phone or computer.
  2. Search for **@TripMazerBot**.
  3. Send the `/start` message shown in **Settings → Integrations**, including its code. There's a **Copy** button next to it.
</Accordion>

## Open TripMazer inside Telegram

TripMazer also opens as an app inside Telegram, so you can see your trips without leaving the chat. On **@TripMazerBot**'s profile, tap **Open App**.

It has four tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Mazer** | Ask Mazer anything, your chats, your morning check-in and your routines |
| **Trips** | Your trips. While you're travelling: today's plan, a Plan B for bad weather, and Live Trip |
| **Imagine** | Photo, video and song templates, plus Story Reels, Mazer Cuts, Memories and Mazer Makes |
| **You** | Your plan, Mazer's personality and voice, AI Memory, Saved Passwords and connected apps |

Telegram's back arrow at the top takes you back a screen. The app follows Telegram's light or dark theme, unless you picked one in TripMazer.

**Imagine.** Pick a template, add a photo of yourself, and Mazer makes the picture in about 15 seconds: golden hour in the place you're going, a vintage travel poster, a magazine cover, 35 mm film, a clay figure and more, plus seasonal ones like Diwali lights. Templates about a place fill in your next trip's destination, and you can change it. **Photo** at the top takes your own idea instead. Save a picture with **Save**, or tap **Share** to send it to any Telegram chat or group, with a **Make your own** button under it. **Post to Story** puts a picture or video on your Telegram Story, and **Send to my chat** drops it into your chat with @TripMazerBot. Pictures count against the same daily allowance as pictures in chat (unlimited on Mazer Pro and Elite).

**Videos.** The **Make it move** row turns your photo into a 4 to 8 second clip: arriving somewhere with a drone pull-back, a golden hour walk, a train window, a night market stroll, a postcard that comes alive, a window seat on approach, or a passport stamp landing. **Video** at the top takes your own idea, and **Make a video of this** sets any of your pictures moving. A video takes a minute or two; you can leave the screen, and it arrives in your chat with @TripMazerBot and in My creations. Each one uses one of your videos for the month (Free 1, Mazer Pro 5, Mazer Elite 18), and Mazer asks before it uses your last one.

**Sticker packs (Mazer Elite).** The **sticker pack** template turns a photo of you into six stickers on holiday in your next trip's destination. The pack arrives in your chat with @TripMazerBot in about a minute, with a button to add it.

**Songs.** The **Songs for the road** row writes and sings a song about your trip: a trip anthem, a road trip singalong, a goodbye to the place, a Bollywood road trip number in Hinglish, your trip as a rap, or a song for someone back home that sings their name. Lo-fi and departure-lounge jazz are instrumental. Mazer writes the lyrics from your trip's real stops and anything you add, then records two versions with cover art in a minute or three. **Song** at the top takes your own idea in a style you pick. Each song uses one of your songs for the month (Free 1, Mazer Pro 5, Mazer Elite 18). Use photos of yourself, or of people who said yes.

**Signing in.** If your Telegram is already linked, you're signed in straight away. If it isn't, enter your email and the 6-digit code we send you. That signs you in (or creates your account) and links Telegram, so next time you go straight in. Google sign-in doesn't work inside Telegram: use the email address of your Google account instead.

## Buy Pro or Elite in Telegram

Inside Telegram, [Mazer Pro and Elite](/plans) are paid with **Telegram Stars**, Telegram's own currency for apps and bots. Send `/upgrade` to @TripMazerBot, or open TripMazer and go to **You → What Pro and Elite add**. You'll see your current plan and each plan's price in Stars, with a **Monthly** or **Yearly** choice.

- **Monthly** renews every 30 days from your Stars balance. Turn renewal off any time with `/upgrade` → **Turn off renewal**, or in Telegram under **Settings → My Stars**. You keep the plan until the 30 days end.
- **Yearly** is one payment for 365 days. It doesn't renew.
- If you don't have enough Stars, Telegram offers to top up as you pay.
- The plan is on your TripMazer account, so it works on the web and in the app too.
- If you already pay for a plan on the web, Telegram shows that plan and sells you nothing more. On the web, a plan paid in Stars is managed in Telegram.

Problems with a payment? Send `/paysupport` followed by what happened. It goes straight to the TripMazer team, and if we can't deliver what you paid for, we refund the Stars in full.

<Note>
  Chatting with Mazer in the Telegram chat itself needs [Mazer Pro or Elite](/plans).
</Note>

## Slash commands

Type `/` in the chat to see them all, or send `/help`. The most useful:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/app` | Open the TripMazer app: trips, Mazer and Imagine |
| `/today` | What's happening today |
| `/day` | A specific day's itinerary |
| `/trips` | List your trips |
| `/trip` | Choose the active trip by name |
| `/newtrip` | Create a new trip |
| `/flights`, `/hotels`, `/activities` | Search for the active trip |
| `/budget` | Budget summary |
| `/bookings` | Your hotel bookings |
| `/docs` | Trip documents and tickets |
| `/remind`, `/reminders` | Set and view reminders |
| `/translate` | Translate text |
| `/interpret` | Two-way live interpreter, for example `/interpret English Thai` |
| `/negotiate` | Bargaining help with fair prices and cultural tips |
| `/phrases` | An offline phrase pack for a language or destination |
| `/journal`, `/wrapped`, `/stats` | Trip highlights, Trip Wrapped cards and lifetime stats |
| `/share` | A share link for the trip |
| `/new` | Start a fresh conversation (keeps trips, memory and watches) |
| `/upgrade` | Your plan, and Pro or Elite in Telegram Stars |
| `/paysupport` | Help with a Stars payment |
| `/unlink` | Unlink Telegram |

[Routines](/concepts#routines) you save also get their own command.

## Use Mazer in a Telegram group

Add **@TripMazerBot** to the group chat for a shared trip, then link the group to the trip with `/linktrip`. Mention Mazer, or reply to it, when you want it to answer.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/linktrip`, `/unlinktrip` | Link this group to a trip, or unlink it |
| `/who` | Trip members |
| `/status` | Trip status and countdown |
| `/group` | Group trip status and members |
| `/invite` | An invite link to join the trip |
| `/polls`, `/newpoll` | View, vote on and create group polls |
| `/split` | A split itinerary when the group wants different things |
| `/me` | Your share and your flights, shown privately |

## Unlink Telegram

Send `/unlink` to the bot, or go to **Settings → Integrations → Telegram** and choose **Disconnect**. Your trips and memory stay in your account.
