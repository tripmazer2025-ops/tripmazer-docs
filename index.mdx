## What TripMazer is

TripMazer is a travel app built around **Mazer**, an AI companion you talk to the way you'd talk to a well-travelled friend. Ask for a long weekend in Goa, a honeymoon in Bali or a work trip to Dubai, and Mazer:

- builds the day-by-day plan around your pace, food rules and budget
- searches real flights, hotels and things to do, and books flights and hotels inside TripMazer
- keeps the trip in one place: bookings, documents, budget, packing and the people coming with you
- travels with you on **WhatsApp**, **Telegram** and **voice**, and (on Elite) watches your flights for delays and gate changes
- turns the photos and days afterwards into a journal, a story and a reel

You don't need to learn menus. If you can describe what you want, Mazer can usually do it, and it asks before anything that books, pays, sends or deletes.

## What a trip looks like

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask">
    Tell Mazer where, when and who's coming. It answers with ideas, prices and a plan you can push back on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it a trip">
    Say "save it" and the plan becomes a trip with its own home: days, bookings, budget, documents and packing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it its days">
    Mazer fills the itinerary and checks each day for clashes, closed places and long transfers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and book">
    Book flights and hotels in TripMazer's checkout, or (on Pro) let Mazer import bookings you've already made from Gmail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take Mazer along">
    On the trip, Mazer is in your messaging app: today's plan, expenses from a receipt photo, flight alerts and a check-in to family.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Already have an account? Open [tripmazer.com/app](https://tripmazer.com/app) and type **"How does this work?"**. Mazer answers with your own next step and can replay the tour.
</Tip>

## Where Mazer works

| Where | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| [Web app](/install/web-and-iphone) | Planning, the trip dashboard, checkout, memories |
| [iPhone](/install/web-and-iphone#add-tripmazer-to-your-iphone-home-screen) | The web app from your home screen (there's no App Store app yet) |
| [Android app](/install/android) | Push notifications, share-to-chat, Trip mode, widgets |
| [WhatsApp](/install/whatsapp) and [Telegram](/install/telegram) | Everything on the move: quick questions, receipts, voice notes, alerts |

Every channel shares the same account, trips and memory. Start a plan on your laptop, and ask about it on WhatsApp at the airport.
