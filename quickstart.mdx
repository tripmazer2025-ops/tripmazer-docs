You need a TripMazer account (free, no card) and a trip in mind. Everything below happens in the web app; you can do the same on WhatsApp or Telegram once they're connected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [tripmazer.com/auth/signup](https://tripmazer.com/auth/signup) and sign up with Google, Facebook, Microsoft or email.

    You'll land in a short setup called your **traveller passport**: passport country, home city, food rules and allergies, interests, budget style, pace and who you usually travel with. It takes two minutes and Mazer reads it on every channel, so it's worth filling in.

    <Note>
      Your passport country lets Mazer check visa rules without asking, and food rules and allergies are treated as strict. You can change any answer later at [tripmazer.com/welcome](https://tripmazer.com/welcome).
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask for a trip">
    Open [tripmazer.com/app](https://tripmazer.com/app) and describe the trip in one message. The more of **where, when, who and budget** you include, the better the first answer.

    <Prompt
      title="Try this"
      text="Plan 5 days in Bali from Bengaluru, 12–16 December, for two of us. Relaxed pace, we love food and beaches, budget around ₹1.5 lakh total."
    />

    Mazer replies with a day-by-day plan, real prices where it searched them, and photos of the places it mentions. Push back in plain words: "swap day 3 for something quieter", "fewer temples", "cheaper hotel".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save it as a trip">
    When the plan looks right, say **"save it"**. Mazer creates the trip with its destination, dates and budget, and copies the day-by-day plan into the itinerary.

    If Mazer doesn't know your exact dates or total budget yet, it asks for the one missing detail, once.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Look around your trip">
    Open the trip from **Trips**. Each trip has a **Trip Home** (what's next, the days, and an Ask Mazer box) plus screens for **Plan**, **Bookings**, **Docs**, **Budget**, **Group** and **Saved**.

    On **Plan**, each day has a **Day check** that flags clashes, tight connections, places that are closed when you arrive and overfull days, with one-tap fixes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book or import">
    Ask Mazer for flights or hotels. On a flight or hotel card, **Book** opens TripMazer's own checkout: travellers, extras, then payment. Mazer never pays on its own.

    Already booked somewhere else? Forward the confirmation email to Mazer on WhatsApp or Telegram, upload the PDF, or (on Pro) connect Gmail and let Mazer file booking emails into the right trip automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take Mazer along">
    Connect [WhatsApp](/install/whatsapp) or [Telegram](/install/telegram) from **Settings → Integrations**. From then on you can ask "what's on today?", send a receipt photo, or tell Mazer "let Mum know I've landed" from your messaging app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
