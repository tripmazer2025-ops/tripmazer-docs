## What Mazer asks before doing

Mazer asks you first before anything that's hard to take back:

- **Booking and paying.** Mazer never pays. Payment happens only in TripMazer's checkout.
- **Sending** emails, messages or posts, and **publishing** anything.
- **Deleting** anything, in TripMazer or a connected app.
- **Changes in connected apps**, such as calendar events, Sheets, Notion or Todoist.
- **Phone calls** made for you (Elite).

Routines and scheduled tasks never pre-approve any of these. Scheduled tasks only read.

The exceptions are things you switch on yourself:

- **[Automatic check-in](/features/auto-check-in)** (free seats only)
- **Family check-ins** you ask for, like "tell Mum I've landed" (Elite)
- **Itinerary changes in Autopilot**, if you choose that in **Settings → AI Preferences**

## What Mazer can see

- **Your location** only when you share it, allow it in the browser or app, or send a photo that carries it.
- **Connected apps** only once you connect them, and only what those apps allow.
- **Websites** you ask it to use. At a sign-in page, Mazer stops. On Elite, you sign in yourself through a private link, and Mazer never sees what you type.

## Your data

| What | Where to control it |
| --- | --- |
| What Mazer remembers about you | **Settings → AI Memory**: see, change and undo |
| Everything Mazer did recently | **Settings → Activity**: the last 30 days |
| Scheduled tasks | **Settings → Agent Tasks** |
| Connected apps | **Settings → Integrations**: disconnect any time |
| Saved website sessions and passwords | **Settings → Connected Websites** and **Saved Passwords** |
| Your visibility on Mazer Match | **You on Mazer Match**: Discoverable and Ghost mode |
| Face suggestions in Memories | Turn them off from the album's **⋯** menu, which deletes the face data |
| Trip documents on Android | **Lock Docs** puts them behind your phone's screen lock |
| Your account | **Settings → Privacy & Data**, and [data deletion](https://tripmazer.com/legal/data-deletion) |

Read the full [privacy policy](https://tripmazer.com/legal/privacy).

## Being honest about limits

Mazer is built to say what it doesn't know. It won't claim a booking, payment, flight change or file exists until the system confirms it, won't quote a fare that no search returned, and says so when it can't identify a place in a photo.
