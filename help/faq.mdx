<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is TripMazer free?">
    Yes. Free includes full planning, real flight, hotel and activity search, Mazer Live voice and up to 2 active trips, with 20 web chat messages every 5 hours. Pro and Elite add more. Both start with a 7-day trial with no card. See [Plans and limits](/plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Mazer book or pay for anything without asking me?">
    No. Anything that books, pays, sends, publishes or deletes needs your approval first, and payment always happens in TripMazer's checkout. The one thing that runs without asking each time is [automatic check-in](/features/auto-check-in), which you switch on yourself, and even that only takes free seats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an iPhone app?">
    Not yet. Add the web app to your home screen from Safari: it opens full-screen like an app. See [Web and iPhone](/install/web-and-iphone).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an Android app?">
    Yes, in testing on Google Play and opening up gradually. See [Android app](/install/android).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I book flights and hotels in TripMazer?">
    Yes. **Book** on a flight or hotel card opens TripMazer's own checkout. See [Booking flights and hotels](/features/flights-and-hotels).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Mazer know where I am?">
    Only when you share your location (on WhatsApp or Telegram), allow it in the browser or app, or send a photo that has location data. Family updates are about your flights, never your position.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my family follow my flights?">
    On Elite, yes. Say "keep Mum posted on my flights" and she gets an invite. See [Safety and family updates](/features/safety-and-family).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I plan a trip with friends?">
    Yes. Pro allows up to 10 travellers on a trip and Elite up to 20. On Free, you can share a read-only plan. See [Group trips](/features/group-trips).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Mazer remember things between chats?">
    Yes. Your traveller passport is read on every plan, and on Pro and Elite Mazer also remembers what it learns across trips and channels. See and change it in **Settings → AI Memory**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What languages does Mazer speak?">
    Voice replies and Mazer Live have eleven English voices with different accents. The Trip Audio Guide narrates in 18 languages, and the interpreter and phrase packs cover many more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    In **Settings → Billing & Plan**. See the [cancellation and refunds policy](https://tripmazer.com/legal/cancellation-refunds).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account and data?">
    See [data deletion](https://tripmazer.com/legal/data-deletion).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
