## Mazer

**Mazer** is the AI you talk to in TripMazer. It's one assistant with one memory, whether you reach it in the web app, the Android app, WhatsApp, Telegram or by voice.

Mazer doesn't just answer. It can search flights, hotels and activities, change your itinerary, log expenses, set reminders, draft emails, run small calculations, read links and videos you send, and work with apps you've connected. When it does something, it tells you what it did; when it couldn't, it says so plainly.

## Trips

A **trip** is where everything for one journey lives. Mazer creates one when you say "save it" or "make this a trip" and it knows the destination, the exact dates and a total budget (your home city is the default origin).

Creating a trip and planning a trip are two separate choices: "create a trip" only saves it, while "plan the whole trip" runs the full flow of searches and day-by-day planning.

Each trip has a **Trip Home** with these screens:

| Screen | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | The day-by-day itinerary, with a Day check on every day |
| **Bookings** | Flights, hotels and activities, including ones imported from email |
| **Docs** | Tickets, vouchers, visas and IDs, per traveller |
| **Budget** | Cost by category against your budget, spending and who owes whom |
| **Group** | Who's coming, roles, polls and the group chat |
| **Saved** | Places and experiences you've shortlisted |

Packing, Memories, Trip Wrapped and Live Trip open from the Trip Home too.

## Memory

Mazer remembers you in two layers:

- **Your traveller passport**: the profile you fill in at sign-up (passport country, home base, food rules, interests, budget style, pace, body clock, who you travel with). Mazer reads it on every channel. Food rules and allergies are treated as strict.
- **What it learns over time**: facts you mention ("my partner is vegetarian", "I hate red-eye flights"), past trips and preferences. You can see and delete these in **Settings → AI Memory**.

Because memory is shared, something you tell Mazer on WhatsApp is known on the web, and the other way round.

## Approvals

Mazer asks before anything that's hard to take back. Anything that **books, pays, sends, publishes or deletes** appears as a card for you to approve first, on every channel. Mazer never pays for you: payment always happens in TripMazer's checkout.

Changes to your own plans are lighter. Itinerary edits arrive as a card to apply, or apply on their own if you choose **Autopilot** in **Settings → AI Preferences**. The other levels are Minimal, Balanced and Proactive.

<Note>
  Saving something as a [routine](#routines) never pre-approves it. A routine that books or sends still asks each time.
</Note>

## Channels

You can reach Mazer in five places. They share one account, one set of trips and one memory, but each is good at different things.

| Channel | Good at |
| --- | --- |
| **Web app** | Planning, the trip dashboard, checkout, memories, settings |
| **Android app** | Push notifications, share-to-chat, Trip mode, widgets |
| **WhatsApp** and **Telegram** | Quick questions on the move, receipts, voice notes, alerts, group chats |
| **Mazer Live** | Real-time voice, hands-free |

A few things only happen on the web, such as checkout payment, the itinerary's Day check buttons and the Memories editor. On the bots, Mazer sends you a link to the right screen instead.

## Routines

When you find yourself asking Mazer for the same thing often, say **"save this as a routine"**. Mazer proposes the steps from the last few hours with a name and trigger phrases, and saves it after you approve. Saying the phrase later runs it again (on Telegram it also gets its own `/command`).

You can keep up to 20 routines, managed in **Settings → Skills**. For watches, errands and scheduled tasks, see [Watches, errands, routines and tasks](/features/watches-routines-and-tasks).

## Connected apps

In **Settings → Integrations** you can connect Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Docs and Sheets, Notion, Canva, Todoist, Zoom, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, YouTube and Instagram, and your own MCP servers. Once connected, Mazer can read from them and, after your approval, act in them: add a flight to your calendar, file a booking email, export a budget to Sheets. See [Connected apps](/features/connected-apps).
