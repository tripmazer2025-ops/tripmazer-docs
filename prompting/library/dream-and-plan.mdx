## Choosing where to go

<Prompt
  title="Shortlist destinations"
  text="Where should we go in [March] for [5] days from [Bengaluru]? Two of us, around [₹80,000] in total, and we like [food and easy hikes]. Give me three options with rough costs and why each fits."
/>

<Prompt
  title="Compare two places"

## Building the plan

<Prompt
  title="A full first plan"
  text="Plan [7] days in [Japan] from [Delhi], [3–9 April], for [two adults]. [Relaxed] pace, budget around [₹3 lakh] total."
  why="Covers all five essentials, so Mazer can search and plan in one go."
/>

<Prompt
  title="Let Mazer run the whole flow"
  text="Plan the whole trip."
  why="After a trip is saved: Mazer searches flights, stays and things to do, then fills the days."
/>

<Prompt
  title="Save what you've worked out"
  text="Save it."
  why="Creates the trip and copies the day-by-day plan from this chat into its itinerary."

<Prompt
  title="Fix a packed day"
