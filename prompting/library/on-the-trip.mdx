## The day

<Prompt title="Today" text="What's on today?" />

<Prompt
  title="Tired day"
  text="I'm tired. Lighten the rest of today."
  why="Mazer moves heavier stops to a lighter day or skips them, keeps meals, bookings and rest, and changes nothing until you tap to apply."
/>

<Prompt title="Running late" text="We're running an hour late. Rework the rest of today." />

<Prompt title="Rain plan" text="It's raining in [Ubud]. What indoor swaps make sense for this afternoon?" />

## Around you

<Prompt
  title="Food near me"
  text="Somewhere good for a vegetarian lunch near me, under [₹1,000] for two."
  why="Share your location first on WhatsApp or Telegram, or allow location on the web."
/>

<Prompt
  title="What is this?"
  text="What is this building?"
  why="Send it with a photo. Mazer reads any signs, checks places near you, and says so if it can't confirm the name."
/>

<Prompt title="Read a menu" text="Translate this menu and tell me what's vegetarian." />

<Prompt
  title="Fair price"
  text="What's a fair taxi fare from here to [the airport], and how should I bargain?"
  why="On Telegram, /negotiate starts a bargaining helper."
/>
