Every prompt here works as written. Words in **[brackets]** are placeholders: swap in your own destination, dates and names.

**Try in Mazer** opens the web app with the prompt already typed in the chat box. It isn't sent until you press send, so you can fill in the brackets first. You can also paste any of these into WhatsApp or Telegram.
