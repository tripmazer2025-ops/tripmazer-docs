Flights and hotels book inside TripMazer: **Book** on a card opens our checkout, and Mazer never pays on its own. Things to do link to partners such as Viator, Tiqets and Headout.

## Flights

<Prompt
  title="Search a return trip"
  text="Find flights from [Bengaluru] to [Bali] on [12 December], back on [16 December], for 2 adults. Morning departures, no red-eyes."
  why="Mazer searches each leg separately, so you see the best options both ways."
/>

<Prompt
