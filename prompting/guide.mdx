You don't need special phrasing to use Mazer. Talk to it like a well-travelled friend who can also book things. A few habits make a big difference, though, because they give Mazer what it needs to act instead of asking you questions.

## 1. Lead with the five essentials

Most trip requests need the same details. Give the ones you know in your first message, and Mazer can search and plan in one go.

| Essential | Example | If you leave it out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where** | *Bali*, *somewhere warm in Europe* | Mazer suggests options first |
| **When** | *12–16 December*, *a long weekend in March* | Prices and availability are estimates |
| **Who** | *two adults and a 6-year-old* | Rooms, fares and pace may not fit your group |
| **Budget** | *around ₹1.5 lakh in total* | Mazer plans anyway, and asks once at the end before saving |
| **From** | *from Bengaluru* | Your home city from your passport |

Your pace, interests, food rules and travel style come from your [traveller passport](/concepts#memory), so you don't need to repeat them.

<CodeGroup>
```text Vague
Plan a trip to Bali.
```

```text Better
Plan 5 days in Bali from Bengaluru, 12–16 December, for two of us. Relaxed pace, we love food and beaches, budget around ₹1.5 lakh total.
```
</CodeGroup>

## 2. Ask Mazer to do it, not to explain how

Mazer can act: add a stop, move a day, search fares, set a reminder, log an expense. Phrase requests as actions and it will do them, instead of describing how you could.

<CodeGroup>
```text Advice
What do you think about visiting Tanah Lot?
```

```text Action
Add Tanah Lot to day 3 for sunset, and move the cooking class to day 4.
```
</CodeGroup>

Anything that books, pays, sends or deletes still comes back as a card for you to approve. See [Approvals](/concepts#approvals).

## 3. Say "save it", or "plan the whole trip"

These are two different requests, on purpose.

- **"Save it"** or **"make this a trip"** creates the trip and copies the plan from your chat into its itinerary. It doesn't start new searches.
- **"Plan the whole trip"** runs the full flow: flights, stays, things to do and a day-by-day plan.

Use the first when you've already worked the plan out in chat, and the second when you want Mazer to take it from a blank page.

## 4. Correct in small steps

You don't need to rewrite your request when something's off. Reply with just the change, in plain words, and Mazer adjusts what it already made.

<CodeGroup>
```text Starting over
Plan 5 days in Bali again but with cheaper hotels and less temples and a quieter day 3.
```

```text Small steps
Cheaper hotels, please.
Fewer temples overall.
Make day 3 a quiet beach day.
```
</CodeGroup>

## 5. State hard limits as limits

Your trip budget guides what Mazer picks first, but it's never a hidden filter. If something is a real limit, say so, and Mazer will filter on it.

<CodeGroup>
```text Soft
Find us a nice hotel in Seminyak.
```

```text Hard limit
Hotels in Seminyak under ₹8,000 a night, free cancellation, walking distance to the beach.
```
</CodeGroup>

When everything costs more than your budget, Mazer tells you in real numbers and suggests fewer nights, other dates or a cheaper area. It won't quietly drop the options.

## 6. Name the trip when you have more than one

With several trips saved, say which one you mean: "on my Japan trip", "for Goa". On Telegram, `/trip` sets the active trip so you don't have to repeat it.

## 7. Bring what you already have

You don't need to retype anything. Mazer reads:

- **Links**: Instagram reels, YouTube and TikTok videos, articles and hotel pages
- **Photos**: receipts, menus, signs, buildings, screenshots of bookings
- **Files**: booking PDFs and confirmation emails
- **Voice notes**, on WhatsApp and Telegram
- **Your location**, for "near me" questions

<Prompt
  title="Example"
  text="Here's a reel: [paste link]. Pull out the places and add the good ones to my Bali trip."
/>

## 8. Ask for the format you need

Mazer can answer as a table, a checklist, or a file. It writes and runs small programs for the maths, so totals and splits are calculated, not guessed.

<Prompt
  title="Example"
  text="Make me an Excel file of every expense on my Goa trip, by category, with a chart of spending per day."
/>

Mazer can make PDFs, Excel and CSV files, Word documents, calendar (`.ics`) files, QR codes and charts.

## What Mazer won't do

Knowing the boundaries saves time:

- **Pay for you.** Payment always happens in TripMazer's checkout.
- **Change a flight ticket itself.** It checks what a change would cost and who can make it, then helps you request it.
- **Change hotel dates itself.** It prices the change; you complete it in **Bookings → Change dates**.
- **Guess.** If it can't confirm a fact, such as a place in a photo or a fare that no search returned, it says so instead of inventing one.
- **Act on your connected apps without asking.** Sending, publishing and deleting always need your approval.
