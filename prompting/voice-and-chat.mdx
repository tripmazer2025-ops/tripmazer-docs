## Talking to Mazer Live

- **Talk normally.** Full sentences work better than keywords. "Find us somewhere quiet for dinner near the hotel" beats "dinner hotel quiet".
- **Interrupt whenever you like.** Mazer stops and listens.
- **Answer confirmations clearly.** Before any change, Mazer reads it back. Say **"yes"** or **"no"**. "Sure, maybe" leaves it unsure.
- **Spell codes slowly.** For booking references and flight numbers, say them letter by letter, or type them instead.
- **Steer the delivery.** "Slower", "shorter answers", "switch to the Sydney accent". It changes straight away.
- **Show it.** Turn on Live Lens and point your camera at the menu, sign or board you're asking about.

## Voice notes on WhatsApp and Telegram

Voice notes are often the fastest way to ask on the move. Mazer transcribes them, answers, and can reply with its own voice note in your chosen voice.

<Note>
  If voice notes come back transcribed in the wrong script, check your voice language in **Settings → AI Preferences**.
</Note>

## Chatting on WhatsApp and Telegram

- **Short is fine.** "Hotel address?" works when you're on a trip; Mazer knows which one.
- **Choose the trip once.** With several trips, say "on my Japan trip", or use `/trip` on Telegram to set the active one.
- **Send things, not descriptions.** A receipt photo, a forwarded booking email, a reel link or your location is quicker than typing it out.
- **Start fresh when a topic changes.** On Telegram, `/new` clears the conversation but keeps your trips, memory and watches.
- **Use buttons when they appear.** Approvals, "Lighten my day" and check-ins come with one-tap answers.

## Group chats

In a Telegram group linked to a trip:

- Mazer answers when you **@mention** it, reply to one of its messages, or use a `/command`. It stays quiet otherwise.
- Group answers use the whole group's trip, so "what's tomorrow?" means the group's plan.
- `/me` shows your share of costs and your flights privately, so you don't post them to the group.

See [Telegram](/install/telegram#use-mazer-in-a-telegram-group) for setup and the full command list.
