The Mazer API gives your app the travel work search APIs can't do. Each **job** runs on our side, from watching a travel video to placing a call, and reports back with what it found and what it cost.

## Jobs

| Job | What it does | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reel to Trip** | Every place in an Instagram reel, TikTok or YouTube video, checked on the map | Live |
| **Watch a trip** | Delays, gates and cancellations until landing, with the way out already found | Live |
| **Call a business** | Mazer phones a hotel or airline in the local language and returns the answers | Next |
| **Web errand** | A task on a real website, finished with sources and screenshots | Next |
| **Route** | The fastest, cheapest and overnight ways across flights, trains and buses in India | Next |
| **Price watch** | A webhook the moment a fare falls below your line | Next |

Jobs marked **Next** are planned and not callable yet. See [Coming next](/api/coming-next).

The whole API is described in OpenAPI 3.1 at `GET /v1/openapi.json` (no key needed), for client generators and agents.

## How a job runs

1. **Sent:** you POST the job with your API key.
2. **Working:** Mazer does the work on our side. Your app waits on nothing.
3. **Finished:** the result arrives by [webhook](/api/webhooks), or you read it with `GET /v1/jobs/{id}`.
4. **Charged:** only now, and only for a finished job. A job that fails costs nothing.
