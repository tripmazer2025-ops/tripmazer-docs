The Mazer API is pay as you go. There's no subscription or minimum: you add credit, and each finished live job is taken from it. `GET /v1/prices` and the console's **Jobs** page show each job's price in your currency.

- **New accounts** start with free credit, once per person.
- **Test keys** never use credit.
- **A live job** holds its price when it starts and is charged when it finishes. A job that fails releases the hold and costs nothing.
- **At zero**, new live jobs answer `402 insufficient_balance`. Test keys keep working.
- **Credit lasts 12 months** from when it was added.

## Adding credit

In the console, open **Credit & billing → Add credit**. Rupee accounts pay by UPI, card or netbanking; each top-up includes GST and gets a tax invoice by email. Dollar accounts pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Polar, who add any sales tax or VAT and email the receipt; the credit appears within a minute of paying.

## Low credit

When a charge leaves your available credit under its low line, the account's owners and admins get an email and your webhooks get one `wallet.low` event. It won't repeat until you add credit.

To move the line or turn the warning off, open **Settings → Low-credit warning** in the console.

## Daily spend caps

In **Settings → Daily spend caps**, give any live key a most it may spend in a day (UTC). Once a key reaches its cap, new jobs from it get `402 daily_cap_reached` until midnight UTC; jobs already running finish. Test keys never spend, so they have no cap.

## Movements

**Credit & billing** lists every movement: free credit, top-ups, charges with the job each paid for, and refunds. `GET /v1/wallet` returns the same.
