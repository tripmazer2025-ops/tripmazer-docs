title: "Watch a trip"
description: "Send a booking; we watch every flight until it lands and tell you, and the traveller, what changes."
icon: "plane"
---

Watch a trip takes a booking as flight numbers, a pasted itinerary, a booking email or a PDF. Each flight is checked against live status before the trip opens, so a typo can't become a watched trip. From then on every change arrives as an event: on the trip, on your webhooks, and by email to the traveller if you ask.

When a flight is cancelled, diverted or delayed by two hours or more, the trip also gets **other ways there**: flights for that day and the next, ranked by arrival, and trains and buses when both airports are in India.

## Request

```bash
curl -X POST "$BASE/v1/trips?wait=30" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TRIPMAZER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: booking-881" \
  -d '{
    "flights": [{ "number": "EK 501", "date": "2026-11-22" }],
    "traveller": { "name": "Asha Rao", "email": "asha@example.com" },
    "reference": "booking-881"
  }'
```

`POST /v1/trips` is a shortcut for `POST /v1/jobs` with `"kind": "watch_trip"`. Send exactly one of:

| Field | What to send |
| --- | --- |
| `flights` | Up to 8 flights: `number` like `EK 501` and `date`, the **local** departure date |
| `text` | An itinerary as pasted, GDS lines included (`6E 2134 BLR-DEL 03 Dec 07:45`) |
| `email` | `{ "subject", "from", "body" }`, where `body` is the booking email's text or HTML |
| `pdf` | `{ "base64", "filename" }`, the booking PDF, up to 5 MB |

Optional: `traveller` (`name`, `email`), `notify_traveller` (on when an email is given), and `reference`, your own id, sent back with every event.

## Response

The job's `result` is the trip:

```json
{
  "id": "trip_c81fef0e45cb41c2aed8bba8df2770bb",
  "object": "trip",
  "mode": "live",
  "status": "watching",
  "reference": "booking-881",
  "page_url": "https://tripmazer.com/trip-status/SGq-HTHnvMQljP6AMsSqe4Bt",
  "updates_in_chat": { "whatsapp_url": "https://wa.me/…?text=TRIP%20236F9F05", "telegram_url": "https://t.me/TripMazerBot?start=trip_236F9F05", "whatsapp": false, "telegram": false },
  "flights": [{
    "number": "EK501", "airline": "Emirates", "date": "2026-11-22", "status": "scheduled", "delay_minutes": 0,
    "from": { "iata": "BOM", "city": "Mumbai", "terminal": "2", "gate": null, "utc_offset": "+05:30" },
    "to": { "iata": "DXB", "city": "Dubai", "terminal": null, "gate": null, "baggage_belt": null, "utc_offset": "+04:00" },
    "departure": { "scheduled": "2026-11-21T22:45:00+00:00", "estimated": null, "actual": null },
    "arrival": { "scheduled": "2026-11-22T02:10:00+00:00", "estimated": null, "actual": null }
  }]
}
```

Times are instants in UTC; `utc_offset` gives each airport's local time. `page_url` is a page you can share with the traveller: it carries your account's name, their first name, the flights and what happened, and refreshes itself.

If a flight can't be found on that date the job fails with `flight_not_found` and nothing is charged.

## Events

Each change is stored on the trip and sent to your [webhooks](/api/webhooks):

| Event | When |
| --- | --- |
| `trip.watching` | The trip opened |
| `flight.delayed` | Departure or arrival moved; `data.delay_minutes` is the new delay |
| `flight.gate_changed` | A terminal, gate or baggage belt was assigned or changed (`data.from`, `data.to`) |
| `flight.cancelled` | Cancelled; `trip.options` follows |
| `flight.diverted` | Diverted; `trip.options` follows |
| `flight.departed` | Took off, with `actual_departure` when the flight data has it. If the data never shows the departure, it comes just before `flight.landed` with `inferred: true` |
| `flight.landed` | Landed, with `actual_arrival` when the flight data has it. Airports can be slow to report a landing: 30 minutes after a flight's own arrival time, if the rest of its record agrees, it comes with `inferred: true`, `actual_arrival: null` and `estimated_arrival` (usually within a few minutes) |
| `trip.options` | Other ways there: `data.flights` and `data.ground` |
| `trip.completed` | Every flight has landed, or the watch ended |

Each webhook carries `trip_id`, your `reference`, `flight` (the flight now) and `data`.

## Updates in a chat

Besides email, travellers can get the same updates on **WhatsApp** or **Telegram**. Only the traveller can turn this on: messaging apps don't let anyone be messaged first. Each trip carries two links in `updates_in_chat`, also shown on the trip page and in the first email:

| Link | What the traveller does |
| --- | --- |
| `whatsapp_url` | Opens WhatsApp with a short message ("TRIP 236F9F05") to send to TripMazer's number |
| `telegram_url` | Opens TripMazer's Telegram bot; they tap **Start** |

Updates arrive under your account's name, with a link to the trip page. Replying **STOP** turns them off. `updates_in_chat.whatsapp` and `.telegram` say whether a chat is linked.

## Read, test and stop

| Request | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/trips` | Your recent trips for the key's mode |
| `GET /v1/trips/{id}` | The trip now, with its events, newest first |
| `POST /v1/trips/{id}/options` | Other ways there for one of its flights, now |
| `POST /v1/trips/{id}/simulate` | Send a pretend `delayed`, `cancelled`, `diverted`, `gate_changed` or `landed` through the trip |
| `POST /v1/trips/{id}/stop` | Stop watching |

A simulated event is recorded and sent like a real one with `"simulated": true`; the flight's stored status doesn't change. Option searches and simulations are limited to 10 a trip a day.

<Tip>
With a test key you get a simulated trip: its gate, a delay, take-off and landing play out over the next few minutes, so you can build your webhook handler without waiting for a real flight. Test trips never email anyone.
</Tip>

## How often we look

Each flight is checked a day and six hours before departure, three hours and one hour before, after take-off, and every 15 minutes from shortly before landing until it lands. A trip ends three hours after its last arrival with no word.
