---
title: "Airport brief"
description: "An airport and the traveller's cards in; the lounges they open, from the banks' own lists, with terminals."
icon: "couch"
---

Airport brief lists an airport's lounges and says, for each, whether the traveller's cards get them in: **free**, **likely**, **restricted** (an airline's or cabin's own lounge), **other side** (domestic when the flight is international, or the reverse), or **not listed**. **Free** means the lounge is on the issuer's published list for that card, with the source and the date it was checked. **Likely** means there is no list to check: for Priority Pass, LoungeKey and DragonPass, not every lounge is in the programme, so check its app.

```bash
curl -X POST "$BASE/v1/jobs?wait=30" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TRIPMAZER_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "kind": "airport_brief", "input": { "airport": "BLR", "cards": ["Axis Magnus", "HDFC Infinia"], "side": "domestic" } }'
```

| Input | |
| --- | --- |
| `airport` | IATA code |
| `cards` | Up to 10 cards as people name them ("Axis Magnus", "Priority Pass"); none is fine |
| `terminal`, `side` | Optional: `departures`, `arrivals`, `domestic` or `international` |

The result has the airport, the cards it recognised (and any it didn't, with what they might be), up to 12 lounges with their verdict, the terminal or `area` when the name or address says, the card that opens them, visits and spend rules, and terminals with gates and essentials where we hold the map.

<Note>Card names are matched exactly to a bank's product; a card we don't hold the terms for comes back in `cards.unmatched` rather than as a guess.</Note>
