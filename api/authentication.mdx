---
title: "Keys and modes"
description: "Test and live API keys, how to send them, and how to revoke one."
icon: "key"
---

Every request carries a key in the `Authorization` header:

```text
Authorization: Bearer tm_test_…
```

`X-Api-Key: tm_test_…` works too.

| Key | Starts with | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Test** | `tm_test_` | Runs every job at once with sample results. Never uses credit |
| **Live** | `tm_live_` | Runs the real job and charges your credit when it finishes |

- Make and revoke keys in the console under **API keys**. A key is shown once, when you make it; we keep only a hash.
- Only owners and admins of the account can make or revoke keys.
- A revoked key stops working at once and answers `401 unauthorized`.
- Each key may send up to 120 requests a minute.

The **Test** and **Live** switch at the top of the console shows that mode's runs and request log. Credit is always the live balance.
