## The console, page by page

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The day: who's waiting for a person, what the assistant got ready for an OK, and a timeline of everything it did |
| **Inbox** | Customers who asked for a person. Reply right in their chat, then hand it back to the assistant |
| **Pipeline** | Every lead from **New** to **Quoted**, **Accepted**, **Paid**, **Travelling** and **Done** (or **Lost**), with its owner, tags, notes and tasks. Sending and accepting a quote, and a first payment, move the lead for you |
| **Conversations** | Every chat your assistant has had with a customer |
| **Customers** | Everyone who talked to your assistant, with the name, phone and email they gave. **Download CSV** exports the list |
| **Quotes** | Quotes and bookings. See [Quotes and bookings](/agencies/quotes-and-bookings) |
| **Packages** | The trips your assistant offers, at your prices |
| **Broadcasts** | Offers and news by email to the customers who'd want them, chosen by stage or tag, with templates. Each email carries an unsubscribe link (owners and admins) |
| **Suppliers** | Hotels, drivers and guides with their rates, and your requests to them. See [Suppliers](/agencies/suppliers) |
| **Performance** | Enquiries, quotes, bookings, money in and margin over a period, and how the assistant is doing (owners and admins) |
| **Your assistant**, **Jobs**, **Channels** | Who your assistant is, what it does on its own, and the website chat (owners and admins) |

The console works on a phone too: the sidebar folds into a menu at the top left.

## Team and roles

Add people in **Settings → Team** by the email of their TripMazer account. If they don't have one, ask them to sign up at [tripmazer.com](https://tripmazer.com) first.

- **Owners** and **admins** see everything, including setup, billing, broadcasts and sales figures.
- **Agents** see the day-to-day work: Home, Inbox, Pipeline, Conversations, Customers, Quotes, Packages and Suppliers.

To share new enquiries out fairly, turn on **Share new enquiries in turn** in **Settings → Team**: each new customer goes to the person with the fewest open leads. Starter includes 2 people and Growth 5.

Each person sets their own name and photo in **Settings → Your profile**. Someone in more than one agency switches between them at the top of the sidebar.

## Alerts

Each person chooses how they hear about things in **Settings → Notifications**:

- **Email**
- **Telegram**, from the TripMazer bot, once Telegram is linked to their TripMazer account
- **Phone**, in the TripMazer Android app

Alerts cover a customer waiting for a person, a quote accepted, a deposit reported, customers' passport details, a supplier's answer, and a rating after a trip. Owners and admins also get the morning brief.

## Developers

On Growth and Custom, **Settings → Developers** holds API keys and webhooks, so your own systems hear when a conversation starts, a trip or booking is made, a booking's status changes, or a customer asks for a person.
