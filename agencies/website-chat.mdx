Your assistant answers on your own website, in your agency's name. Customers ask about trips, and it offers your packages first, at exactly the prices you wrote. It never makes up a package or changes a price, and it never mentions TripMazer.

## Put it on your website

1. Open **Channels** and copy the **Install code**, one line.
2. Paste it into your site where you'd add analytics. WordPress, Wix, Shopify or your own site all work.
3. Enter **Your website** and the console checks that the chat is live there.

Not ready for your own site yet? Try it on the [demo site](https://tripmazer.com/widget/demo.html) first.

## How it looks and behaves

In **Channels**:

- **Look:** the **Colour**, the **Button text** and the **Button position** (left or right). Your logo comes from **Settings → Company**.
- **Behaviour:**
  - **First message:** what the chat says when it opens.
  - **Ask visitors who they are:** ask for a name and a phone or email before the chat starts, ask but let them skip, or don't ask. Every chat becomes a customer in your console either way.
  - **Sites that may show the chat:** list your own domains so copies on other sites are turned away.

In **Your assistant**, choose the currency it quotes in, the markup it adds to hotels and flights it books live (your packages keep their own prices), your team hours, and a monthly spending cap.

## When a customer asks for a person

A customer can tap **Talk to a person**, or simply ask for one. Then:

1. Everyone on your team is alerted by email, Telegram and the TripMazer app, as each person chooses in **Settings → Notifications**.
2. The conversation waits in your **Inbox**. Open it, reply, and the customer sees your answer in the same chat under your first name. The assistant stays quiet while you're in it.
3. Hand the chat back when you're done, and the assistant picks up again, knowing what you said.

Outside your team hours, the customer is told when you're back, and the assistant keeps helping until then. It never promises a callback you didn't set.
