## Your suppliers and their rates

In **Suppliers**, add the hotels, transport, guides and activity operators you book, with a contact, an email and what they charge you. A rate you save comes up as you type a line in the quote editor, and under **Add a supplier rate**. It comes in as your cost, priced with your default markup and converted into the quote's currency if it's in another. See [Quotes](/agencies/quotes-and-bookings#prices-per-traveller-and-your-costs).

## Ask suppliers

Your assistant can email suppliers for you, in your agency's name, and bring their answers back:

- **Is it available, and at what rate?** In **Suppliers → Requests**, choose **Ask suppliers**, tick one or more suppliers, and say what you need, the dates and the guests. Each gets an email with a link. Their answers sit side by side, so you can compare.
- **Please confirm this booking.** On a booked item with a supplier and no confirmation number, choose **Ask … to confirm**. The supplier answers with the number, and it goes straight onto the booking and the customer's voucher.

Suppliers answer on a page with your name on it, with no account and no login, or simply reply to the email, which comes to your own address.

When an answer arrives, your team is alerted and it shows on Home. From an answer with a rate, **Save as a rate** adds it to that supplier's rates for your quotes.

A supplier needs an email address in **Suppliers** before you can ask them.

## Reminders

A supplier who hasn't answered in a day gets one polite reminder: on Home for your OK, or sent automatically if you choose. After five days without an answer, the request is marked **No answer** and leaves the waiting list. You can also **Remind** or **Cancel** a request yourself. Cancelling closes it on the supplier's page; the email itself can't be unsent.
