## Start the trial

1. Go to [tripmazer.com/agencies](https://tripmazer.com/agencies) and choose **Start your 7-day trial**.
2. Enter your agency's name and website, and, if you like, one package you sell. You can add more later.
3. Sign up, or sign in with an existing TripMazer account.

The trial includes 50 conversations and needs no card. When it ends, your assistant pauses until you choose a plan. Nothing is deleted. See [Plans and billing](/agencies/plans-and-billing).

## Log in to your console

Your console is at [tripmazer.com/business](https://tripmazer.com/business). From the agencies page, use **Agency log in** in the header, or go to **tripmazer.com/business/login**: it signs you in and takes you straight to the console. If you already run an agency, **Start free trial** takes you to your console instead of a second sign-up.

## Set up, step by step

**Home** shows a checklist until your first real customer arrives:

1. **Name your assistant** in **Your assistant → Who it is**, and describe how it should come across, in your own words.
2. **Add your brand** in **Settings → Company**: logo, colour, how your PDFs look, your seal and signature, and how customers reach you (WhatsApp, phone, email). These appear on the chat, quotes, invoices, vouchers and trip pages.
3. **Add your packages** in **Packages**, or import them from a brochure. The assistant offers these first, at exactly the prices you write.
4. **Put the chat on your website** from **Channels**. It's one line of code. See [The website chat](/agencies/website-chat).
5. **Invite your team** in **Settings → Team**.

Already keep customers in a spreadsheet? Bring them across from **Customers → Import**, and past bookings from **Import** in **Quotes → Bookings**. See [Bring your customers and bookings across](/agencies/team-and-customers#bring-your-customers-and-bookings-across).

Use **Try** on Home to talk to your assistant as a customer would, before anyone else does.

<Tip>Set your **Team hours** in **Your assistant**. Outside them, a customer who asks for a person is told when you're back, and the assistant keeps helping until then.</Tip>
