## Packages

Add the trips you sell in **Packages**: a title, destination, nights, a from-price with its currency and whether it's per person, per couple or in total, and what's included. The assistant offers these first, quoting the price exactly as written.

To add many at once, choose **Import from a brochure** and give it a brochure PDF or a page from your website. It proposes the packages it finds; nothing is saved until you tick the ones you want.

Starter includes up to 20 packages; Growth and Custom have no limit.

## Quotes

Write a quote from **Quotes**, or with **Write a quote** from a customer's conversation. A quote can have:

- **flights**, typed in or pasted from your GDS or an airline's email
- a price **per traveller**, for adults, children and infants
- price lines, or lines taken from a package or a supplier's rate
- **options** for the customer to choose between, each with its own price
- a **day-by-day plan**
- what's included and not, and how long the price holds
- a **deposit**, as a percentage

### Flights

Add each flight under **Flights**: the airline, the flight number, the airport codes it leaves from and lands at, and the local times on the ticket. You can also add the cabin, the checked baggage, the terminal and the PNR. The city appears under each airport code, and the flying time is worked out from the airports' time zones, so you never type either.

To save typing, choose **Paste a booking** and paste:

- an Amadeus, Sabre or Travelport screen, read on the page as you paste it
- an airline's confirmation email, which the assistant reads
- simple lines like *EK601 KHI-DXB 15 Nov 11:55 13:20*

Check the flights it reads before you send. A flight that leaves from the airport the last one landed at, within a day, is a connection, and the customer sees the wait between them. A trip that comes back to where it started shows as **Outbound** and **Return**. **Add the return** starts the way back for you.

The customer sees each flight with its times, cities, flying time, airline and PNR on the quote page, in the **PDF** and on the voucher. The **Itinerary PDF** gives the flights a page of their own, four to a page.

### Prices per traveller, and your costs

**Price per traveller** sets a price each for adults (12 and over), children (2 to 11) and infants (under 2). It opens by itself when a quote has flights, counting the travellers you've typed. The customer sees, for example, *2 adults × AED 1,200*. Travellers you count here fill in an empty **Travellers** field.

Mark what each price line is, a stay, a transfer, a visa, insurance and so on, with the small icon at the start of the line. The customer sees the word beside the line.

**Show your costs** adds your cost to each line and each traveller's price, and shows your cost and margin under the total. Costs stay in the console: they never appear on the quote page, in a PDF or on the voucher.

Start typing a line and your suppliers' rates that match it appear underneath, like *water villa* or *Kochi airport*. Choose one, or use **Add a supplier rate**, and the line fills in with the rate as your cost, a price of your cost plus your default markup, and what the line is (a hotel rate is a stay, a transport rate a transfer). A rate in another currency is converted at your exchange rates; if one is missing, you're told which to add.

### Live prices

Turn on **Live prices from TripMazer** in **Settings → Assistant → Prices** to price quotes with today's real rates:

- **Find live hotels**, above the price breakdown: search a place, with dates, travellers, star rating, meals and free cancellation. Each room shows **your cost** (your TripMazer price), the hotel's public price for reference, and the price with your default markup. **Add** puts it on the quote as a Stay line.
- **Find live flights**, above the flights: one way or return, with travellers, cabin and *Direct only*. **Use these** fills every flight leg and the price per adult, child and infant, with baggage and fare details. Each fare is your cost plus your flight markup: your default markup, or one of its own set in **Markup on live flights**, as a percentage or a fee per traveller (infants too). A fee in your currency is converted at your exchange rates for a quote in another currency; if a rate is missing, you're told which to add.

Your customers see your price, never your cost or where the rate came from.

Rates move, so a quote with live rates is checked again **before it goes out**. If a rate moved by more than 2% or is no longer available, you see what changed and choose whether to send it or update it first. It's checked again **when the customer accepts**, and you're told straight away if anything moved. A price on a sent quote never changes by itself.

Your assistant can do the same: *Find a 4-star in Seminyak with breakfast, 12 to 15 Feb, 2 adults*. Each option comes with a code, like H2 or F1, and *Add H2 and F1 to Sara's quote* puts them on, after you press **Yes**. Each agency has up to 100 live searches a day.

### A second currency

**Also show in**, under the total, shows the price in a second currency as well, for example for a customer abroad. The rate fills in from your exchange rates, and you can change it for this quote. It's saved with the quote, so the figure never moves after you send it. The quote page, the PDF and the Itinerary PDF show, for example, *About AED 2,737 at 1 AED = 22.65 INR*, and under each option's price when there are options.

Keep your exchange rates in **Settings → Assistant → Prices**: one line per currency, such as *1 USD = 83.2 INR*. Type the rates you use, or choose **Use today's rates** to start from the day's market rates, then change any of them before you save.

### Draft the days

Once a quote has a title or a destination, choose **Draft the days** above the day-by-day plan. The assistant writes one day for each day of the trip from the dates (or the package's nights, if there are no dates yet; five days if neither), the destination, the travellers, what's included and your notes: arrival on day 1, departure on the last day, and real places in a sensible order. Edit any day before you send.

The draft never includes prices. It names a hotel, airline or tour company only if your own text names it; otherwise it says "your resort" or "a private driver". If the quote already has days, the assistant asks before replacing them, and the button reads **Redraft the days**.

Send it by copying the link, on WhatsApp, by email, or straight into the customer's website chat. The customer opens a page with your logo, chooses an option and accepts it, and you're told the moment they do. You can see when a quote was opened, and a quote you change after sending becomes a new version.

**PDF** makes a branded PDF of the quote, with its flights, its options or price, the day-by-day plan and what's included, plus a link to accept it online. On a computer it downloads; on a phone it opens the share sheet so you can send it straight on WhatsApp. Choose how it looks in **Settings → Company → Brand**: **Classic**, **Bold** (your colour across the top) or **Minimal** (your logo centred, no colour band). The voucher PDF takes the same look.

On the Growth and Custom plans, and during a trial, the button is **Itinerary PDF**: a photo itinerary in your colours, with a cover, a page for the options and what's included, a photo for each day and your contact details at the end. Day photos are chosen from map data when you use **Draft the days** or **Add photos**: the place a day names, or one of the destination's scenic spots. Map photos print their photographer's credit. To change a day's photo, select it to pick another, search for a different place or upload your own.

The cover is the first and last page. Without a cover, the PDF uses a photo of the destination. To choose your own, select the cover photo next to **Where** in the quote. You can pick a map photo of the destination, search for another place or upload a photo of your own.

## Deposits

Deposits go straight to you: add your UPI ID, or a payment page of your own (Razorpay, PayU or your bank's), in **Settings → Company → How customers pay you**. The quote shows the amount and how to pay. When the customer taps **I've paid**, your team is alerted to check and record it. TripMazer never holds your customers' money.

### Payments that record themselves

Connect your own Razorpay or Stripe account in **Settings → Integrations → Payments**, and customers can pay online:

- **From the quote**, once they accept it: **Pay online** asks for the deposit, or the whole price if the quote has no deposit.
- **From their trip page**, which lists the balance still to pay.
- **From a link you send**: **Copy payment link** on a booking. The link always asks for what's owed when it's opened, so an old link never asks for the wrong amount.
- **From balance reminders**, which link to the same page.

When a customer pays, the money goes straight to your account. The payment is recorded on the booking with its reference, and the booking is made from the quote if there isn't one yet. Your team is alerted, and the customer gets a receipt in your name. A payment is recorded once, even if the customer pays and closes the page straight away.

To connect:

- **Razorpay:** paste the key ID and key secret (**Account & Settings → API keys**). Then, recommended, add a webhook in Razorpay with the address shown, tick *payment_link.paid* and *refund.processed*, and paste its secret back. Without the webhook, a payment still records when the customer comes back from paying, or within the hour.
- **Stripe:** paste the secret key (**Developers → API keys**). Payment notifications are set up for you.

Test keys work for trying it out, and the pay page says so. Only owners and admins can connect or disconnect. A refund made in Razorpay or Stripe alerts your team, so you can record it on the booking.

## Booking terms

Add your payment and cancellation terms once, in **Settings → Company → Your booking terms**. They're printed in your quote PDFs; the Itinerary PDF gives them a page of their own. On the quote page they sit under **Booking terms**, and the customer is told that accepting means agreeing to them. A blank line starts a new paragraph, and a short first line, like *Cancellations*, prints as its heading in the Itinerary PDF.

Your assistant answers payment, cancellation and refund questions from these terms. Anything they don't cover, it leaves to your team to confirm with the quote.

## Seal and signature

Add your company seal and a signature in **Settings → Company → Seal and signature**, with who signs (*Anita Rao, Director*). A photo or scan of the ink on plain white paper works: the paper is taken out, so the seal sits on the page as a real stamp does.

They're printed where a paper document is signed: *For* your agency, the signature on the line with the seal beside it, then *Authorised signatory*. Choose which documents carry them: quotes (the quote page, its PDF, and the Itinerary PDF beside **How to confirm**), invoices, and vouchers (the voucher page and its PDF). A cancelled invoice is never signed.

## Bookings

From an accepted quote, choose **Make a booking**. Half an hour after you add it, the customer gets **You're booked** with their trip page, as the **Trip companion** job says. A booking holds:

- **What's booked:** each stay, transfer, flight or activity, with its supplier, your cost and its confirmation number. These make up the customer's **voucher**, which shows no prices. The voucher also lists the flights from the quote, with their PNRs.
- **Payments:** money in from the customer, and out to suppliers. The first payment in moves the lead to **Paid**.
- **Balance due by:** when the rest is due. Leave it empty to use the days before travel set in **Settings → Company → How customers pay you**. With **Balance reminders** on in **Jobs**, the customer is reminded a week before and on the day.
- **The margin:** what the customer pays, less your costs. With a default markup set in **Your assistant → Prices**, the booking suggests what the customer pays from its costs, and **Use** fills it in.
- **Travellers & visas**, the customer's **trip page**, and **Files** for tickets, visas and insurance. See [Passports and trip pages](/agencies/passports-and-trip-pages).

Bookings you already keep in a spreadsheet can come across with **Import**. See [Bring your customers and bookings across](/agencies/team-and-customers#bring-your-customers-and-bookings-across).

Use **Voucher**, **Copy voucher link** or **Voucher PDF** to share the voucher. The PDF carries your branding and every confirmation number, without prices. Add an item's supplier and use **Ask … to confirm** to have the supplier send the confirmation number themselves. See [Suppliers](/agencies/suppliers).

## Invoices

On any booking, choose **Make an invoice** to give the customer a GST invoice.

The first time, an owner or admin adds the agency's invoice details: legal name, address, GSTIN and an invoice prefix. With a GSTIN, the invoice is a **tax invoice**; without one, it's a plain invoice with no tax.

For each invoice:

- **Bill to:** the customer's name, plus their GSTIN if they're a business.
- **Place of supply:** your own state means CGST and SGST; another state means IGST. A customer's GSTIN sets it for you.
- **Lines:** pick a kind of line to fill in the SAC and GST rate (a tour package at 5%, for example), or type your own. Tick **Amounts include GST** if your prices already include it.
- **TCS collected:** for an overseas package, if you collected tax at source.

Invoices are numbered in order through the financial year, like **INV/26-27/0001**, and can't be edited once made. An owner or admin can cancel one; it stays on record marked cancelled, and its number isn't used again. **Open** shows the invoice, ready to save as a PDF. **Link** copies a link for the customer.

TripMazer prepares the invoice; the rate, SAC and place of supply are yours to decide. Check them with your accountant.

### Export to Tally

On **Bookings**, an owner or admin chooses **Export to Tally**, picks a month or any dates, and downloads two files for TallyPrime:

1. **Customers and suppliers:** a ledger for each customer invoiced or paid, with their GSTIN and state, and each supplier you paid.
2. **Invoices and payments:**
   - Each invoice becomes a sales entry, with CGST, SGST or IGST and TCS on their own ledgers.
   - Each payment received goes against its booking's invoice; anything above the invoice goes on account.
   - Each payment to a supplier goes in too. You can turn this off.

In TallyPrime, open your company, choose **Import → Masters** with file 1, then **Import → Transactions** with file 2. Import each period once, then check the Day Book.

Under **Your names in Tally**, set the names your accountant uses for the sales, GST, TCS, bank and cash ledgers. These ledgers must already exist in Tally; the export lists the ones it needs. Cancelled invoices and payments in other currencies are left out, and the export says which.


### Zoho Books

In **Settings → Integrations → Accounting**, an owner or admin chooses **Connect Zoho Books** and signs in to the agency's own Zoho account. From then on, every ten minutes (or straight away with **Sync now**):

- **Invoices** go to Zoho under their own numbers, with the customer, place of supply and GST. Cancel an invoice here and it's voided in Zoho, unless a payment is already on it; then you're told to sort it out in Zoho.
- **Payments received** go to Zoho, applied to their invoice. Anything over the invoice stays as the customer's credit.
- **Customers** are matched by name, or made in Zoho the first time.

Choose where to start with **Send invoices and payments from**. Under **Taxes in Zoho**, pick which of your Zoho taxes each GST rate uses, within your state and for other states. TripMazer suggests the obvious ones. If your Zoho Books isn't set up for GST, invoices without GST still go, and invoices with GST wait until you turn it on in Zoho.

Invoices with TCS and payments in other currencies don't go to Zoho; add them yourself. Anything that didn't go is listed with Zoho's reason.
