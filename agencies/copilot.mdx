Open the copilot with **⌘J** (Ctrl+J on Windows), the **Ask** button at the top of the sidebar, or your assistant's initial in the top bar on a phone. It answers from your agency's own data, with links to each customer, quote and booking, and only shows you what your role can already see.

## What to ask

- "Who needs a reply from us today?"
- "Who still owes us money before they travel?"
- "Which quotes were opened but not accepted?"
- "Whose passports or visas need sorting?"
- "What did Anjali ask about?"
- "How did we do this month?" (owners and admins)
- "Write a WhatsApp message to Ravi about his Bali dates." It writes it for you to copy; it never sends messages itself.

## What it can do for you

It can **add a note**, **add a task** (with a due date and a person), **move a lead** to another stage, **draft a quote** from a customer's conversation, **write a quote** you describe, **record a payment**, and **ask suppliers** for availability and rates.

To write a quote, describe it the way you'd say it: *"Quote Sara Khan Dubai to London on Emirates EK 1, 13 Dec at 14:30, back on EK 2 on 20 Dec, 2 adults at 2,400 AED each, Business."* It sets out each flight, the price per traveller and any other lines, such as a hotel, and saves the quote as a draft for you to check. It never makes up a time, flight number, price or PNR. When it says a customer paid, it records the payment against their booking and tells you what's still due.

It never does any of these on its own. Each comes back as a card with **Do it** and **Not now**, and most can be undone afterwards for a week. See [Undo](/agencies/assistant-jobs#undo).

When a name matches more than one customer, it lists them with what tells them apart, so you can say "the one with the Munnar quote".

## On Telegram

Your assistant works from the TripMazer bot on Telegram too, typed or as a voice note, so you can quote from your phone.

1. Link Telegram to your TripMazer account in **TripMazer settings**, if you haven't for alerts already.
2. Send **/agency** to the TripMazer bot. If you're on more than one agency's team, pick one.
3. Ask or describe anything you would in the console. A voice note is written out first, so you can see what it heard.

Each change comes back with **Yes, do it** and **No**, as in the console. Once a quote is drafted you get **Preview**, **Open in TripMazer**, **PDF** (the Itinerary PDF, on Growth and Custom) and **Mark as sent**, which marks it sent and gives you a message with its link to forward to the customer.

It answers as you, with what your role can see. Send **/personal** to go back to your own Mazer.

## Limits

Each agency can ask up to 300 questions a day, and each person up to 150. Sales figures are for owners and admins.
