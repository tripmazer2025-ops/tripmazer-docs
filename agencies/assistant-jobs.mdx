Every 15 minutes your assistant looks over your agency and does the jobs below. You choose each job's level on the **Jobs** page (owners and admins). **Ask first** puts the work on Home for one click; **Send** lets the assistant act alone.

| Job | What it does | Starts on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft quotes from enquiries** | When a chat says where, roughly when and who's going, drafts a quote from your packages. Prices only come from a package, worked out for the group (per couple, per person or in total); children are left for you to price. With no match, it leaves prices blank and says so | Draft |
| **Follow up on quotes** | A quote not opened in a day, or opened and not accepted in two, gets one short email in your agency's name | Ask first |
| **Passports and visas** | For trips in the next 90 days, checks every traveller and flags problems. From 45 days out, asks customers for missing passport details, and again two weeks before | Ask first |
| **Trip companion** | Once you add a booking, sends the customer **You're booked**: their trip page, what's paid and what's left, and the passport form if the trip needs one. Sends the trip page again 1 to 3 days before departure, and asks how it went 1 to 3 days after they're back. Imported bookings get no **You're booked** | Ask first |
| **Balance reminders** | When a booking still has money owed, reminds the customer a week before the balance is due and on the day, with your UPI ID or payment page. The due date is the booking's **Balance due by**, or the days before travel set in **Settings → Company → How customers pay you**. With neither, no reminder goes | Ask first |
| **Chase suppliers** | One reminder to a supplier who hasn't answered in a day; after five days the request lapses | Ask first |
| **Morning brief** | Between 8:30 and 11 your time, owners and admins get the day: who's waiting, new enquiries, drafts to send, opened quotes, deposits to check, passports to sort, supplier answers, money due and who travels. Quiet mornings send nothing | On |

Each job can also be turned **Off**.

## Ready for you

What the assistant got ready waits on **Home**, under **Ready for you**, with one button each:

- a drafted quote: **Review and send** opens it in the quote editor, or **Discard draft**
- an email waiting for an OK: **Send the email**, read it first with **See the email**, or **Not now**
- a passport or visa problem, or a supplier's answer: **Open booking**, or **Got it**

Problems that are fixed, and drafts that are sent, leave the list by themselves.

## Undo

You can undo what the assistant changed for seven days: notes and tasks it added, a lead it moved, a quote it drafted, supplier requests it sent, and a confirmation number a supplier's answer filled in. Use **Undo** on Home, in the copilot, or in the log on the **Jobs** page.

If someone on your team has changed the thing since, it stays as they left it. Emails can't be unsent, so they have no undo.

## The log

The bottom of the **Jobs** page lists everything the assistant did, newest first: what it drafted, sent, flagged or was asked to do, and what was undone.
