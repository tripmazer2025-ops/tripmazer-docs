TripMazer for Agencies gives a travel agency its own assistant. It carries your agency's name, logo and colour, offers your packages at your prices, and never mentions TripMazer to your customers. You name it yourself; the examples here use **Isla**, the assistant of a sample agency, Aster & Isle.

It works in two places:

- **With your customers:** a chat on your website, a page for each quote, a passport page and a trip page for each booking.
- **With your team:** the console at [tripmazer.com/business](https://tripmazer.com/business), where you reply to customers, send quotes, track bookings and payments, and decide how much the assistant does on its own.

## What it does on its own

Beyond answering customers, the assistant works for your team. Every 15 minutes it looks over your agency and:

- drafts a quote when a customer has said where, roughly when and who's going
- follows up on quotes that weren't opened, or were opened and not accepted
- checks every traveller's passport and visa before the trip
- sends customers their trip page before they leave, and asks how it went after
- reminds suppliers who haven't answered your requests
- sends owners and admins a morning brief

By default nothing goes to a customer or supplier until someone on your team clicks. You set how far each job goes on the **Jobs** page, and you can undo what it changed for a week. See [The assistant's jobs](/agencies/assistant-jobs).
