## Travellers and visas

Each booking has a **Travellers & visas** section. For every traveller, it shows their passport country and expiry, and what they need for the destination:

- **Passport validity**, by the destination's rule: valid for three months after leaving the Schengen area, for the whole stay in the US, UK and a few others, and six months after the trip everywhere else.
- **Visa:** no visa needed, visa on arrival, an e-visa or ETA, or a full visa, and whether the trip is longer than visa-free entry allows.

Each visa fact names its source. Official government rules come first, with the date they were checked. Otherwise the check uses a community passport dataset, marked "confirm with the embassy". Always confirm before your customer travels.

Set the destination country on the booking. Until you do, the check uses the country in the trip's name. Record each traveller's visa as not needed, needed, applied or granted; **granted** clears the warning.

## Let customers add their own details

Use **Copy link** or **WhatsApp** in **Travellers & visas** to send the customer their passport page. They add each traveller's passport country and expiry, and a photo of the passport if you want one, and see straight away if a passport needs renewing. Your team is alerted when they send details. Only your team can open the passport photos.

## The trip page

Every booking has a trip page for the customer, in your brand. Open it with **Trip page**, or share it with **Copy trip link** or **WhatsApp it**. It shows:

- a countdown, then **Today** during the trip
- the plan day by day, with every booked stay, transfer and activity on its day, and each confirmation number
- before they leave: passport details, the official arrival card where one applies, and the voucher
- emergency numbers and money tips for the country, where TripMazer has them, and your contact details
- a chat with your assistant, which knows the booking and can answer "what's our hotel's confirmation number?" at any hour. The chat appears when the booking is linked to a customer, as bookings made from an accepted quote are.

After the trip, the page asks the customer for a rating from 1 to 5 and a note. You're alerted, and the rating shows on the booking. Customers who give 4 or 5 are pointed to your review page, if you add one in **Settings → Company → How customers reach you**.
