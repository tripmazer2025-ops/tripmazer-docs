Every agency starts with a **7-day free trial** with 50 conversations and no card. A conversation is one customer's chat in a day. When the trial ends, your assistant pauses until you choose a plan; your packages, customers and conversations stay where they are.

Prices are on [tripmazer.com/agencies](https://tripmazer.com/agencies#plans).

## What each plan includes

| | Starter | Growth | Custom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conversations a month | 300 | 1,000 | Agreed with you |
| Team | 2 people | 5 people | As many as you need |
| Packages | Up to 20 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Branding | Your name and logo, with a small "Powered by TripMazer" | Your name and logo only | Your name and logo only |
| API and webhooks | No | Yes | Yes |

Every plan includes the website chat, quotes, bookings, suppliers, the assistant's jobs, the copilot, passports and visas, and trip pages.

## Choose or change a plan

Owners and admins manage the plan in **Settings → Plan**. Before the first paid plan, an owner or admin accepts the [Agency Terms](https://tripmazer.com/legal/agency-terms) and the [Data Processing Agreement](https://tripmazer.com/legal/agency-dpa) for the agency:

- **In rupees**, monthly or yearly, paid through Razorpay by card or UPI, plus 18% GST. Add your GSTIN for a GST invoice.
- **In US dollars**, monthly or yearly, paid by card through Polar, which adds local tax where it applies.

**Cancel plan** stops the next renewal; your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

## Extra conversations and spending

Conversations beyond your plan's allowance are charged for each calendar month: on rupee plans they're added to your next Razorpay bill, with GST; on dollar plans Polar charges them at the end of your billing period. To keep the assistant's own costs in check, set a **Monthly spending cap** in **Your assistant**. When it's reached, the assistant pauses until the 1st and asks customers to contact you directly.

For more conversations, more branches or a custom setup, write to [partners@tripmazer.com](mailto:partners@tripmazer.com).
