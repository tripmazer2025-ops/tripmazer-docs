## Reports

**Reports** is for travel admins and finance. Choose a month, or **The last 12 months**, to see:

- what was **booked through TripMazer**, after anything that came back from cancellations
- **expenses claimed**
- what went **outside the policy**, and on how many trips
- what was **saved under the policy**
- **rewards earned**, if your company shares savings with travellers

**Booked, month by month** shows the trend over the last year.

### Group, filter and save

Under **Group and filter**, choose **Group by**: person, team, department, cost centre, project, office, entity or city. **Only** narrows it to one or more departments, cost centres, projects, offices or entities. **Save view** keeps the grouping and filters under a name, ready under **Saved views** next time. **Download CSV** gives finance the same figures.

### Rewards

When **Savings rewards** is on (see [More on Policy](/teams/policy-and-approvals#more-on-policy)), each reward a traveller earned is listed under **Rewards**. Your company pays them through payroll; choose **Mark paid** once it has.

### Summaries by email

- **The monthly report:** on the 1st of each month, the owner and finance get last month's travel: trips booked, what was booked and claimed, what went outside the policy and what was saved, the most visited cities and who spent the most.
- **The Monday summary:** each Monday morning, the travel admins get what's waiting on them, who's away that week and what's been booked this month. Turn it off on **Policy**.

Both keep your company's time zone.

For anything the page doesn't show, ask Mazer on the desk in **Company** mode, such as *"Which team spent the most this quarter?"* or *"Who is outside the policy this month?"*. See [Ask Mazer on the desk](/teams/slack-and-mazer#ask-mazer-on-the-desk).

## Your books

Under **Reports → Books**, travel admins and finance send the company's travel spend into its accounts.

### Zoho Books

1. Choose **Connect** and sign in to your company's own Zoho account.
2. If it has more than one organisation, choose which one to use.
3. Set **Send from**: the first date to send.

From then on, every ten minutes, or straight away with **Sync now**:

- **Bookings** made through TripMazer go to Zoho as travel expenses.
- **Refunds** from cancelled bookings go back against them.
- **Paid claims** go as travel expenses reimbursed to the traveller.

Each goes to the account for its kind of spend under **Setup → GL codes**, matched by the account code, or to Travel Expense if you haven't set one. They're paid through your bank account in Zoho, or **Petty Cash** until you add one. Anything that didn't go is listed with Zoho's reason.

### TallyPrime, in India

For companies in India, **Download for Tally** gives the period you picked on **Reports** in two files: a masters file with the Travel Expenses ledger, and the vouchers. In TallyPrime, import the masters file first, then the vouchers. Ledger names come from **Setup → GL codes**.

<Tip>You can also ask Mazer, in **Company** mode, to *send this month to Zoho Books*, or in India to *make October's file for Tally*. It comes back as a card, and nothing happens until you choose **Confirm**.</Tip>
