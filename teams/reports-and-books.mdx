## The Overview

Travel admins and finance open the desk on **Overview**: the year on one screen, in your company's currency. Choose **This year** (your budget year so far) or **Last 12 months**. **Download CSV** gives finance every figure behind it. If trips are waiting for a decision, a button at the top takes you to **Approvals**; a link to a particular trip still opens it there.

- **The headline** says what was spent and how that compares with the same point last year.
- **Month by month** stacks each month's flights, hotels, ground travel (cabs, trains and mileage) and other expenses, with last year's month as a thin line. Point at a month for its figures, or choose **Table**.
- **Where it went** splits the period into those four parts. Spend is what was booked through the desk, less refunds, plus claimed expenses; a booking's own bill isn't counted twice.
- **Saved, and how** adds up three things, and each line opens to the trips behind it:
  - **Under the policy cap:** for trips inside the policy, the cap minus what was paid.
  - **Prices that fell before booking:** the price approved minus the lower price booked.
  - **Airline credits used:** credits from cancelled flights used on a later flight instead of paying again.

  Rewards travellers earned for booking under their allowance are listed below the total.
- **Carbon** counts the CO₂e of booked flights by route, team or month, using the UK government's (DESNZ) 2025 conversion factors: the great-circle distance plus 8%, by cabin and distance. A second figure includes non-CO₂ effects, which the source calls uncertain. Flights count in the month they fly, and cancelled flights aren't counted.
- **How the desk is used:** how long people take to decide a trip (the median, with every decision on a strip), how many were approved at once or went to the travel admin, each team's share of booked spend outside the policy with the reasons travellers gave, and colleagues' ratings of hotels and airlines. A hotel two colleagues rate 4 or more is suggested as preferred, and a travel admin can mark it there.

Ask Mazer on the desk can answer from the same figures: "What did we save this year, and how?" or "How much carbon did our flights make this year?"

## Reports

**Reports** is for travel admins and finance. Choose a month, or **The last 12 months**, to see:

- what was **booked through TripMazer**, after anything that came back from cancellations
- **expenses claimed**
- what went **outside the policy**, and on how many trips
- what was **saved under the policy**
- **rewards earned**, if your company shares savings with travellers

**Booked, month by month** shows the trend over the last year.

### Group, filter and save

Under **Group and filter**, choose **Group by**: person, team, department, cost centre, project, office, entity or city. **Only** narrows it to one or more departments, cost centres, projects, offices or entities. **Save view** keeps the grouping and filters under a name, ready under **Saved views** next time. **Download CSV** gives finance the same figures.

### Rewards

When **Savings rewards** is on (see [More on Policy](/teams/policy-and-approvals#more-on-policy)), each reward a traveller earned is listed under **Rewards**. Your company pays them through payroll; choose **Mark paid** once it has.

### Summaries by email

- **The monthly report:** on the 1st of each month, the owner and finance get last month's travel: trips booked, what was booked and claimed, what went outside the policy and what was saved, the most visited cities and who spent the most.
- **The Monday summary:** each Monday morning, the travel admins get what's waiting on them, who's away that week and what's been booked this month. Turn it off on **Policy**.

Both keep your company's time zone.

For anything the page doesn't show, ask Mazer on the desk in **Company** mode, such as *"Which team spent the most this quarter?"* or *"Who is outside the policy this month?"*. See [Ask Mazer on the desk](/teams/slack-and-mazer#ask-mazer-on-the-desk).

## Your books

Under **Reports → Books**, travel admins and finance send the company's travel spend into its accounts.

### Zoho Books

1. Choose **Connect** and sign in to your company's own Zoho account.
2. If it has more than one organisation, choose which one to use.
3. Set **Send from**: the first date to send.

From then on, every ten minutes, or straight away with **Sync now**:

- **Bookings** made through TripMazer go to Zoho as travel expenses.
- **Refunds** from cancelled bookings go back against them.
- **Paid claims** go as travel expenses reimbursed to the traveller.

Each goes to the account for its kind of spend under **Settings → Trip fields and codes → GL codes**, matched by the account code, or to Travel Expense if you haven't set one. They're paid through your bank account in Zoho, or **Petty Cash** until you add one. Anything that didn't go is listed with Zoho's reason.

### TallyPrime, in India

For companies in India, **Download for Tally** gives the period you picked on **Reports** in two files: a masters file with the Travel Expenses ledger, and the vouchers. In TallyPrime, import the masters file first, then the vouchers. Ledger names come from **Settings → Trip fields and codes → GL codes**.

<Tip>You can also ask Mazer, in **Company** mode, to *send this month to Zoho Books*, or in India to *make October's file for Tally*. It comes back as a card, and nothing happens until you choose **Confirm**.</Tip>
