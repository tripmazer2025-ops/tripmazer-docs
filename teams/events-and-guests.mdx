## Events

An event is an offsite or a conference run through the desk as one thing: a place, dates, a budget and the people going. It's on **Events**, for travel admins to set up and for everyone they invite.

### Set one up

1. A travel admin chooses **New event** and gives it a name, the city and venue, the first and last day, and, if you like, a **Budget for everyone's travel** and an **Up to, a person** cap. Add a cost centre or project, and choose **Who approves it**.
2. **Invite** colleagues and guests.
3. Choose **Send for approval**. With no other approver, **Approve and tell people** approves it at once.

Invited colleagues hear about it by email and in their chats once it's sent for approval, and again when it's approved.

### One approval for everyone's travel

Once the event is approved, each person who's coming books their own travel on **Book**: the event's link fills in the city and dates. A trip is approved with the event, with no one else to ask, when:

- it arrives no more than two days before the event starts, and leaves on its last day or up to two days after;
- it's within the cap a person, or, if the event has no cap, inside the traveller's policy;
- the event's budget has room for it.

Anything else goes through your usual approvals. Changing an approved event's dates, or raising its budget or cap, sends it back for approval.

### Who's coming and arrivals

The event shows who's coming, who hasn't answered and who isn't coming, with each person's trip. **Arrivals** lists the coming flights by day, with the time each one lands, so whoever meets people at the venue knows who to expect when.

### Room blocks

For ten rooms or more, a travel admin can **Ask for a room block**: one request to the supplier's group desk, with the dates, the number of rooms and the most a room a night, instead of booking room by room. The group desk replies by email with what it can hold and the price.

## Guests

A guest is someone you book for who doesn't have a TripMazer account: a candidate, a client or a speaker. Travel admins and colleagues who book for others add them from **Book** (**For: Add a guest…**) or an event's **Invite**, with:

- their name, as on their ID;
- their email and phone, where their tickets go;
- their date of birth, gender and nationality, when an airline needs them.

A guest travels under the policy of the colleague who books for them, and their trip is listed under that colleague with the guest's name. Their tickets and hotel voucher go to the guest's email, and to the colleague who booked. Passport details aren't kept on the desk; they're given at booking, for the airline only.

Guests aren't counted on the Team plan, which counts employees who travel. See [Plans and billing](/teams/plans-and-billing).
