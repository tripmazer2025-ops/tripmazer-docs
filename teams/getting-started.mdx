## Set up your company

1. Go to [tripmazer.com/teams/start](https://tripmazer.com/teams/start). Sign in with your work email, or create an account; you come back to the form.
2. Enter your **Company name** and choose your **Country**. The **Currency** and **Time zone** follow it, and you can change either.
3. Add your **Work email domain**, like *kestrel.in*, so anyone with an email there can join with one link. You can add it later.
4. In India, you can add your **GSTIN**.
5. Choose **Set up the travel desk**. You're the company's owner, and the desk opens on **People**.

Every new company gets a 30-day free trial with everything on, and no card is needed. See [Plans and billing](/teams/plans-and-billing).

## Country, currency and time zone

What you choose shapes the whole desk:

- **Currency:** every amount on the desk, in your policy and in Mazer's messages about work trips. A new company's policy starts in its own currency, with rounded amounts for you to adjust on **Policy**.
- **Time zone:** "today" on the desk, reminders to approvers, the Monday summary, the monthly report and the note the day before a flight all keep your company's clock.
- **Country:** flights and hotels are priced from your country, and it decides what counts as abroad for hotel caps, approvals and passport checks.

The desk is set up for companies in India, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait, Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Switzerland, the United States and Canada.

To change them later, a travel admin opens **Setup → Country, currency and time zone**. Changing the currency doesn't convert amounts a travel admin set themselves: a grade still on the starting policy moves to the new currency's starting amounts, and you're asked to check any grade you've changed.

<Note>GST (reading it off bills, whether the company can claim it, and the GST list), GSTINs and states on legal entities and offices, and the TallyPrime export are for companies in India. Zoho Books and the payroll file work everywhere.</Note>

## Invite your people

Travel admins add people on **People**, in three ways:

- **Invite by email:** type or paste the addresses, choose a grade and a manager for them, and choose **Send invites**. TripMazer emails each person their own link; if an email doesn't go, **Copy link** and send it yourself. **Add from a CSV file** takes every address in a file. With a header row, the file can also carry each person's department, cost centre, office, employee ID, manager's email and grade, and you see who's new and who's updated before you choose **Import**.
- **Join link for your domain:** save your **Work email domain** and post the link where your team will see it. Anyone who signs in with an email on that domain can join.
- **Staff list:** keep the desk in step with where you already keep your staff. Choose a shared sheet (Google Sheets or a CSV link, shared so anyone with the link can view, with an **Email** column and any of **Name**, **Department**, **Cost centre**, **Office**, **Manager**, **Employee ID** and **Status**), Microsoft Entra or Google Workspace. Every morning, new joiners get the invite email, changed departments, managers and cost centres are updated, and people who left are listed for you to remove. Turn on **Remove people who left, without asking** to have them removed. Owners are never removed.

An invite lasts 30 days. **Invited, not joined yet** lists the open ones, with **Email again**, **Copy link** and **Cancel**.

When someone opens their link, they sign in or create a TripMazer account and choose **Join**. Their personal trips, chats and memories stay theirs; the company sees only the work trips they ask for.

## Grades, managers and roles

In **Your team** on **People**, a travel admin sets each person's:

- **Grade:** which policy applies to them. See [Grades](/teams/policy-and-approvals#grades).
- **Manager:** who approves their trips. People without a manager go to the travel admin chosen on **Policy → Who approves**.
- **Finance:** tick it for anyone who handles claims and payments.
- **Details:** their department, cost centre, office and employee ID, and **Books trips for colleagues**, for an assistant who books on others' behalf.

The owner also chooses each person's role: **Traveller** or **Travel admin**. A travel admin runs people, policy and setup, and books approved trips with the company card, so give it to the people who run travel for you. Only the owner makes someone a travel admin or a traveller again. If you change back the travel admin chosen on **Policy → Who approves**, choose another one there.

What each role sees is in [Who does what](/teams#who-does-what).

## Make the desk yours

On **People**, travel admins choose:

- **Logo and colour:** a PNG or JPG under 1 MB, and your colour. The desk, approval emails and e-tickets carry them; Wallet passes take the colour.
- **Look of the desk:** one theme for everyone at your company: **Indigo**, **Midnight**, **Daylight**, **Evergreen**, **Graphite**, **Rosewood** or **Your colour**.

## Set up, step by step

1. Check your country, currency and time zone in **Setup**.
2. Set your grades and who approves on **Policy**.
3. Add departments, cost centres, projects and offices in **Setup**, if you use them.
4. Invite your people on **People**.
5. Ask everyone to fill in **Your details** and tick **Let your travel admins at … book with these**, so their trips can be booked without asking them for their passport each time.
6. Connect Slack on **People**, if your team uses it. See [Mazer in Slack](/teams/slack-and-mazer#mazer-in-slack).

<Tip>Travelling for work yourself? Tell Mazer where and when, the way your team will. See [Ask Mazer in chat](/teams/booking-trips#ask-mazer-in-chat).</Tip>
