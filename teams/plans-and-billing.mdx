Every new company gets a **30-day free trial** with everything on, and no card is needed. When it ends, the desk pauses until the owner subscribes to the **Team plan**. Only the owner changes the plan; travel admins can see it in **People → Your plan**.

## What the Team plan includes

Everything on the desk, for everyone at your company: booking, policy and approvals, expenses and claims, the trip watcher, **Who's where**, savings rewards, reports and your books, and Mazer in Slack and on the desk without using anyone's own message allowance (up to 100 questions a person a day).

**Your plan** shows how many employees have travelled this month so far, and roughly what that comes to.

## Keep the Team plan

### In India

The owner chooses **Keep the Team plan** in **People → Your plan**, during the trial or after it. It's billed after each month for the employees who travelled, that is everyone with a trip booked through TripMazer that month, with a GST tax invoice. Each month's bill is emailed to the owner and listed under **Your plan**, with its tax invoice once it's paid.

A bill is due 15 days after it's sent, and **Your plan** shows the date. The owner is reminded three days before. If it isn't paid by then, the desk pauses until it is; paying it turns the desk straight back on.

### Outside India

The owner chooses **Add a card** in **People → Your plan**. The Team plan is billed monthly in US dollars, by card, for the employees who travel. Sales tax or VAT is added where it applies, and receipts come by email.

You can add the card during the trial: the first charge comes when the trial ends. **Billing and receipts** changes the card, shows your receipts and cancels the plan.

If the plan is cancelled, or a payment fails for good, the desk pauses the same way until a card is added again.

## Live bookings

Bookings on a new desk are virtual: sample fares, and nothing is charged. Once billing is set up as above, a travel admin can turn on live bookings in **People → Your plan → Bookings**. See [Virtual and live bookings](/teams/booking-trips#virtual-and-live-bookings).

## When the desk pauses

When the trial ends without a plan, the plan stops, or a bill is overdue:

- Nobody can request, book or claim trips through the desk, on **Book** or in chat.
- Mazer in Slack only replies that the desk is paused, and questions to Mazer on the desk count against each person's own TripMazer plan.
- Everyone sees **The travel desk is paused** on the desk, and the owner gets a button to turn it back on.

Your people, policy, trips and expenses stay where they are. The owner is emailed a few days before the trial ends, and again if the desk pauses. To turn it back on, the owner keeps the Team plan, adds a card, or pays the overdue bill, in **People → Your plan**.

## Questions on the company

While your company is on its trial or the Team plan, questions to Mazer in Slack and on the desk are on the company, up to 100 a person a day. See [Questions on the company](/teams/slack-and-mazer#questions-on-the-company).

Questions about your plan or a bill? Write to [support@tripmazer.com](mailto:support@tripmazer.com).
