## Ask Mazer in chat

Tell Mazer where and when, on WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or the TripMazer app, the way you'd ask a colleague. Link [WhatsApp](/install/whatsapp) or [Telegram](/install/telegram) first if you'd like Mazer there.

<Prompt title="A work trip" text="I need a work trip from [Bengaluru] to [Pune], [12] to [14 March], for a [client meeting]." />

1. Mazer prices flights and hotels for those dates and checks each against your policy. You get up to three options: the quickest direct flight with the best-rated hotel under your cap, the cheapest flight and hotel, and the quickest flight with a better hotel above the cap. Each says whether it's inside the policy, and if not, why.
2. Tell Mazer which one you want. If it's outside the policy, Mazer asks for a one-line reason for your approver. If your company needs a purpose, cost centre or project on every trip, it asks for those too.
3. Mazer shows what it will send and waits for your yes. The trip then goes to your approver, or is approved at once if your company doesn't need anyone to approve it.

Mazer tells you when it's decided, in your chats and by email. Prices more than a day old are checked again before anything is sent.

## Search on Book

**Book** on the desk is the same trip without the chat:

1. Choose **Flights and hotel**, **Flights** or **Hotel**, and for flights **Return** or **One way**. Fill in **From**, **To** and the dates. **From** starts with your office's city.
2. Pick the **Cabin** (your policy's is marked), **Direct only**, and for hotels which office to stay **Near**, then choose **Search**.
3. Each flight and room is marked **In policy**, or with the reason it isn't. **In policy only** hides the rest.
4. Choose a flight and a room, then **Review**.

Hotels show their photo, stars, guest rating and distance from the office, on a map. You also see how colleagues rated a hotel after their stays, and the hotels a travel admin marked **Preferred**. Each room says whether breakfast is included, whether it has free cancellation (and until when), and that it's paid now, with nothing to pay at the hotel. With savings rewards on, each choice inside the policy shows what you'd earn.

In **Review**, you see the total with taxes and fees. Add what the trip is for, the cost centre and the project, and a reason for the approver if it's outside the policy, then send it.

**Ask Mazer instead** takes your search into chat. **Coming up** lists your next trips and where each stands.

### Book for a colleague

On **Book**, travel admins, and anyone a travel admin marks **Books trips for colleagues** on **People**, choose **For:** and a colleague's name. The trip is the colleague's from then on, and follows their policy and approvals. It shows on your **Trips** as well as theirs.

## Approvals

Whoever needs to decide hears straight away:

- on **WhatsApp** or **Telegram**, with **Approve** and **Decline** buttons, if they've linked either
- in **Slack**, with the same buttons, if your company has added Mazer to Slack
- by **email**, with links to approve or decline
- in the TripMazer app's notifications

On the desk, **Approvals** shows the requests as a pile of cards, one at a time. Each card shows who's going, where and when, the flights and hotel, the policy check and the traveller's reason if it's outside the policy. It also shows the budgets the trip counts against, and which step of the chain it's on. Choose **Approve** or **Decline**, or press **→** or **←**. **Cheaper options** shows what Mazer found for less, when it has something.

For a trip abroad, the approver's message also notes any passport or visa problem, once the traveller has shared their details, and the traveller is told what to fix before they go.

The traveller hears the decision in their chats and by email. If it was declined, they can ask Mazer for other options.

## Virtual and live bookings

A new desk books **virtually**: the fares and rooms are samples from a test account, and nothing is charged. You can follow a trip through approval, booking, tickets and claims before any money moves.

A travel admin turns on live bookings in **Settings → Plan and bookings**, under **Bookings**, once billing is set up: in India, the owner has chosen **Keep the Team plan**; elsewhere, a card is on file. From then on fares are real and booking charges the card entered at checkout. A trip priced on virtual fares needs asking for again to book it live. **Back to virtual bookings** switches back at any time.

## Who books and pays

On **Policy → Payments and expenses**, under **Paying for bookings**, a travel admin chooses one:

- **The travel admin, with the company card** (to start): approved trips wait on **All trips** for a travel admin to book them.
- **The traveller, with their own card:** the traveller, or the colleague who asked, books the approved trip with **Pay and book**. It comes back to them as a claim, already approved with the trip, for finance to pay back. To change or cancel it, they ask a travel admin.

### Book an approved trip

1. Open the trip on **All trips** and choose **Book** on the flights or the stay.
2. Fill in the traveller exactly as on their ID: name, gender, date of birth and nationality, and their passport for a flight abroad. These fill in from **Your details** once the traveller has agreed to share them.
3. Choose **Check the price and hold**. The price is checked again. If it moved and the trip is now outside the policy, nothing is held, and **Send back to** the approver asks them again at the new price.
4. Add a **Checked bag**, if the policy allows one.
5. Choose **Continue to card** and pay in the secure card form.

Once it's booked, the traveller gets the airline's reference or the hotel's confirmation, the e-ticket or hotel voucher in your company's name, a calendar file and a Google Wallet pass, in their chats and by email. On WhatsApp the e-ticket and voucher arrive as PDFs even if they haven't messaged Mazer lately. Passport numbers go to the airline and aren't kept on the booking.

## Your details

So a trip can be booked without asking, each traveller fills in **Your details** once: their name as on their ID, date of birth, nationality, phone, the email for tickets, their passport, seat and meal preferences, frequent flyer numbers and an emergency contact. Then they tick **Let your travel admins at … book with these**.

Travel admins see these details, passport included, only when they book a work trip for that person. The traveller can turn this off at any time, and their personal trips, chats and memories stay with them.

## During the trip

Mazer watches each booked flight from the day before until it lands:

- **The day before**, the traveller gets the booking reference, the terminal, the airport guide and their Wallet pass.
- **The traveller** hears about delays, gate and terminal changes, cancellations, diversions, landing and the baggage belt, in their chats and the app, and by email for delays, cancellations and diversions.
- **The travel admins** hear about the changes that alter plans: delays, cancellations and diversions. A cancellation comes with the next flights inside the policy, and one tap rebooks: it makes a new approved trip, which you book as usual.

If your company has added Mazer to Slack, flight problems are also posted in the desk's channel. To see who hears what, open a booked trip and use **Send a sample delay alert** or **Send a sample cancellation** under **Flight updates**. Each is marked as a sample.

**Who's where** shows travel admins and finance everyone on an approved or booked work trip today, this week or in the next 30 days, taken from the bookings, never from anyone's phone. If **SOS reaches the travel admins** is on, an SOS from someone on a work trip reaches the travel admins too. See [More on Policy](/teams/policy-and-approvals#more-on-policy).

After the trip, the traveller can rate it out of 5 from the trip on the desk, and colleagues see the hotel's score when they book.

## Change or cancel

- **Cancel a booking:** on a booked flight or stay, choose **Cancel**. You see what cancelling would cost first, then choose **Cancel the flights** or **Cancel the stay**, or **Keep it**. The refund shows on the trip.
- **Move a flight:** a travel admin asks Mazer on the desk, in **Company** mode, such as *"Move Priya's return from Mumbai to Friday"*. It finds flights inside the policy for the new date; choose one on the trip, and it becomes a new approved trip that you book as usual. The old flight stays booked until you cancel it.
- **Airline credits:** when a cancelled flight leaves a credit with the airline, record it on the trip. It's kept under **Unused airline credits** on **All trips**, and Mazer reminds you the next time that traveller flies the airline. TripMazer can't apply a credit; use it on the airline's own site.
- **Bookings nobody will use:** travel admins are told when a trip still has bookings after the traveller left the company, after the trip was declined or withdrawn, or when its flights were cancelled but the stay is still booked. Cancel them on the desk for whatever comes back.
- **Change the coding:** travel admins and finance change a trip's cost centre, project or purpose under **Coding → Change** on the trip.
