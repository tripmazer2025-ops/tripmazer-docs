You earn **25% of what each follower you bring pays for Pro or Elite, before tax, for 12 months** from their first payment. The first 25 approved creators earn **30%** while they're active.

## How a follower is credited to you

A follower is credited to you when they open your profile, one of your guides or your follower link, or use your code, and then sign in to TripMazer within **60 days**. It works for a new account, and for an existing one that has never paid for Pro or Elite. The newest creator they came through before signing in is the one credited, and the credit never moves after that.

Commission counts when the follower first pays within **90 days** of being credited, and then on each payment for 12 months.

Your **Links** tab has three ways in:

- **Your profile**: `tripmazer.com/c/yourhandle`
- **Your follower link**: `tripmazer.com/?c=YOURCODE`
- **Your code**, for voice-overs and captions, where a link can't go

Follower links credit sign-ups from **24 December 2026**.

## What earns commission

| Payment | Commission |
| --- | --- |
| Pro and Elite on the web | Yes, on the amount before GST |
| Pro and Elite through Google Play | Yes, on what Google pays us |
| Telegram Stars | No. Telegram pays its own affiliate commission on Stars bought through your Telegram links |
| Credits, top-ups and API usage | No |

Followers get **20% off their first Pro or Elite payment**: by UPI or card in India, and by card outside India.

## Earn your own plan

Being a creator doesn't come with a plan. You earn one from followers who are paying right now:

| Followers paying at once | Your plan |
|---|---|
| 3 | Pro, free |
| 10 | Elite, free |

A follower counts while they're on Pro or Elite, paid on the web or through Google Play. We check every hour. If fewer are paying than your plan needs, it carries on for **up to a month** while you get back there. Elite that drops below 10 becomes Pro after that, as long as at least 3 are paying. **Creator Studio** shows how close you are on the home screen, and we email you when you earn a plan or drop below the line.

If you already pay for Pro or Elite yourself, that plan keeps running. Cancel it in **Settings → Plan and billing** and your earned plan takes over when it ends.

## Getting paid

In **Creator Studio → Earnings**, tap **Add where we pay you** and add:

- your name as it is on your PAN card, and your **PAN**
- a **UPI ID** or **bank account** in that name
- your **GSTIN**, if you're registered for GST (most creators aren't)

We check your PAN and that the account is in your name before your first payout. Your details are only ever shown masked, and you can change them in Earnings.

## When you're paid

Each payment is **held for 30 days**, then it's credited to you. On the **1st of each month** you get a **statement** for the month before, and we pay it by the **10th** once you're owed **₹1,000** or more. A smaller balance carries into the next month.

Open any month in **Earnings** to see the statement: what your followers' payments earned, any refunds, tax deducted, and what was paid to you, with the payment reference. If you're registered for GST, upload your GST invoice for the month on its statement and we pay once it's in.

## Refunds

When a follower is refunded, the commission on that payment is taken back. It shows as **Refunded** in Earnings.

## Tax

For creators in India, we deduct tax at source (TDS) and show it on each statement:

| | TDS |
|---|---|
| Your commission this tax year (April to March) is ₹20,000 or less | None |
| Once it passes ₹20,000 | 2% of the year's commission so far, then 2% of each month's |
| Your PAN isn't linked to Aadhaar | 20% |

The tax is paid to the government against your PAN, counts towards your own income tax, and you get the certificate (Form 16A) each quarter. A free plan you earn is a benefit for tax too: if its value and any other benefits pass ₹20,000 in a year, 10% of that value is deducted from your payout.

Commission is worked out on amounts before GST. Payouts for creators outside India are being set up; what you earn is kept and paid once they start.
