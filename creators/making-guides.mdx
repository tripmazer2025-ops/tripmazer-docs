A guide is one of your own TripMazer trips, published under your handle. You make the trip first, usually from your reel, and then turn it into a guide.

## From a reel

1. In Creator Studio, press **+ New guide**.
2. Paste the reel or video link under **Reel or video link**. Instagram, YouTube and TikTok links work.
3. Press **Plan it with Mazer**. Mazer opens with the link ready to send. It finds every place in the video, checks each one on the map and lays out the days as a trip.
4. Change the trip with Mazer until it's how you'd travel it, then come back to Creator Studio.

## From a trip you already have

1. Press **+ New guide**, then **Use a trip I already have**.
2. Pick the **Trip**, then fill in:
   - **Title**, as followers will see it, like *Goa in 4 days*.
   - **One line about it**, like *Fontainhas, Palolem, Divar, Chapora*.
   - **The reel it came from**, so the guide shows **Watch the reel**.
   - **Cover photo link**. Leave it empty to use the trip's own cover.
3. Press **Save draft**, or **Publish** once you're approved.

Followers get a copy of the trip. Changes you make to your trip later show on the guide; copies followers already made stay theirs.

## Publish, unpublish and delete

Tap a guide's tile, then **Numbers** (or swipe up) to open its numbers:

- **Publish** puts a draft on your profile. Only approved creators can publish.
- **Share link** shares the guide's address.
- **Edit guide** changes its title, line, reel link or cover.
- **Unpublish** moves it back to your drafts. Its address stops working until you publish again.
- **Delete guide** removes it. Copies followers already made stay theirs.

A guide with no cover shows **Add a cover** on its tile, because followers would see a plain tile.

## Each guide's numbers

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Opened** | Times the guide's page was opened |
| **Copied** | Times a follower copied the trip into their account |
| **Signed up** | Accounts credited to you from this guide |
| **Paying** | Of those, followers now paying for Pro or Elite |
| **Earned from this guide** | Your commission from those followers' payments |

## Label your posts

Start the caption of every post that carries your link or code with **#ad**, **#collab** or **#partner**. A note in your bio isn't enough. Followers outside India need the label next to the link too.
