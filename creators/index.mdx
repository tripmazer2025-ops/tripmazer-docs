TripMazer for Creators turns your travel videos into trips your followers can copy. You publish each trip as a **guide** on your own profile at `tripmazer.com/c/yourhandle`. A follower opens it, copies it into their own TripMazer in one tap, and changes it with Mazer. Your original stays as you made it.

When a follower who came through you pays for Pro or Elite, you earn **25% of what they pay, before tax, for 12 months**. See [Earnings and payouts](/creators/earnings-and-payouts).

## Apply

1. Go to [tripmazer.com/creators/start](https://tripmazer.com/creators/start) and sign in. Signing in makes your account if you don't have one.
2. Choose your handle. Use your Instagram or YouTube handle if you can: it becomes your profile address and your follower code.
3. Add the name followers know you by and a line about what you make, then press **Apply**.

You land in **Creator Studio** straight away and can make guides while you wait. We review every application ourselves, usually within a day or two, and email you when you're approved. You can publish once you're approved.

Joining is free.

## What followers see

- **Your profile**, `tripmazer.com/c/yourhandle`: your photo, name and bio, and every published guide as a tile with its copy count.
- **Each guide**, `tripmazer.com/c/yourhandle/guide-name`: your cover, the title, a line about the trip, **Copy this trip**, **Watch the reel** (when you've linked it), and the full day-by-day trip below.

Anyone can open a guide without an account. Copying it takes a free TripMazer account. A follower who signs up from your guide sees on the sign-up page that their account is credited to you.

## Creator Studio

Creator Studio, at [tripmazer.com/creators/studio](https://tripmazer.com/creators/studio), has four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Guides** | Your profile at the top, then your guides and drafts as tiles. Tap one to see it full screen with its numbers. See [Making guides](/creators/making-guides) |
| **Links** | Your profile link, your follower link and your code, each ready to share or copy |
| **Earnings** | What's ready to pay, what's held and what's been paid, payment by payment |
| **Settings** | Your name, bio, photo and links to your Instagram, YouTube and website |

Your handle and code are fixed once you've applied. To change them, write to [partners@tripmazer.com](mailto:partners@tripmazer.com).

## Ask Mazer

Mazer can explain the program and plan a trip from your reel, but it can't approve you, publish a guide or show your earnings from chat. Those live in Creator Studio.
