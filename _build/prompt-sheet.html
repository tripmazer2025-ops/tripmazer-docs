The Mazer prompt sheet

How to get great answers from your AI travel companion

Start with the five essentials

WhereBali, or "somewhere warm"
WhenExact dates if you have them
WhoHow many, any kids
BudgetA total for the trip
FromDefaults to your home city
TryPlan 5 days in Bali from Bengaluru, 12–16 December, for two of us. Relaxed pace, budget around ₹1.5 lakh total.

Phrases that do things

"Save it"Creates the trip and copies the plan into its itinerary
"Plan the whole trip"Flights, stays, things to do and every day
"What's on today?"Today's plan for the trip you're on
"Lighten the rest of today"Eases a tired day; nothing changes until you tap
"Tuk tuk 800 LKR"Logs an expense, shows today's balance
"Send my itinerary to Mum"A ready-to-send message with a link
"Keep Mum posted"Flight updates for family (Elite)
"Save this as a routine"Reuse what you just did with one phrase
"How does this work?"Your next step, and a replay of the tour

Six habits that help

  1. Ask for the action. "Add Tanah Lot to day 3", not "what about Tanah Lot?"
  2. Correct in small steps. "Cheaper hotels." "Make day 3 quieter."
  3. Say hard limits. "Under ₹8,000 a night", "free cancellation".
  4. Name the trip when you have more than one.
  5. Send, don't type: links, photos, receipts, PDFs, voice notes, location.
  6. Ask for the format: a table, a checklist, an Excel or calendar file.

Mazer asks before anything that books, pays, sends or deletes, and payment always happens in TripMazer's checkout.
